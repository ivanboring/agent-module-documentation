Forum Notifications Subscription lets users subscribe to forum notifications, emailing them when new topics or replies are posted.

---

Core Forum has no subscription/notification feature, so users cannot follow a forum or topic. Forum Notifications Subscription adds that: users subscribe and receive emails on new topics or replies. It is a community-engagement feature for sites using core Forum. No unusual security surface; it emails subscribed users about public forum activity. Confirm the email volume is manageable (busy forums generate many notifications) and that subscriptions respect any forum access restrictions.
