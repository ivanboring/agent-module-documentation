Fasttoggle enables fast one-click toggling of settings like published/promoted/sticky on nodes, and status on comments and users.

---

Fasttoggle adds one-click toggle links for common boolean settings — publishing/unpublishing a node,
promoting/demoting, sticky, and status toggles on comments and users — so editors can flip these without
opening the full edit form. It depends on core Node and Comment, is configured at `fasttoggle.settings`,
and provides its own permissions.

Use it to speed up editorial moderation and administration of content/comment/user status. It is an
administration/editing convenience; importantly, each toggle is **gated by the corresponding
permission** (a user can only fast-toggle what they'd otherwise be allowed to change), so it does not
grant new capabilities — it just makes existing ones faster. Confirm the permissions are assigned to the
right roles so the quick toggles match your moderation model.
