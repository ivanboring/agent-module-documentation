Favicons generates and manages favicons/app icons for the site.

---

Favicons **generates and manages favicons and app icons** — producing the various favicon/touch-icon sizes and
the associated markup/manifest from a source image, so browsers and devices show the right icons. It provides its
own permissions, in the SEO package.

Use it to manage site favicons. It is a theming/SEO feature; icons are static assets and it has no content or
access role beyond its permission. Configure the favicon source and outputs.
