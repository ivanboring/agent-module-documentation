Adobe Analytics adds Adobe Analytics javascript tracking.

---

Adobe Analytics adds the **Adobe Analytics (Omniture) JavaScript tracking** to the site — injecting the
Adobe Analytics/AppMeasurement snippet so page views and events are recorded in Adobe Analytics. It provides its
own permissions.

Use it to track traffic with Adobe Analytics. It is an SEO/analytics feature. Privacy/data handling: it loads a
**third-party tracking script** that collects visitor analytics and sends data to **Adobe** — disclose it in
your privacy policy and integrate with consent tooling where required (analytics is often consent-gated under
GDPR). It has no content or access role beyond its permission. Configure the Adobe Analytics settings.
