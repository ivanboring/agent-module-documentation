AdInsight Clarity provides integration for the AdInsight/Clarity tracking.

---

AdInsight / Clarity **adds the Microsoft Clarity / AdInsight analytics tracking script** — injecting the
tracking snippet so visitor behaviour (sessions, heatmaps, recordings) is recorded in Clarity/AdInsight. It
provides its own permissions, in the Tracking package.

Use it to track behaviour with Clarity. It is an analytics feature. Privacy/data handling: it loads a
**third-party tracking script** that records visitor behaviour (potentially **session recordings/heatmaps** —
sensitive) and sends data to Microsoft/AdInsight — disclose it, integrate with consent tooling (behaviour
recording is consent-gated under GDPR), and mask sensitive fields in recordings. It has no content or access role
beyond its permission. Configure the tracking key.
