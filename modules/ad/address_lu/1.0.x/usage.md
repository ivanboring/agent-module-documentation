Address for Luxembourg adds Luxembourg-specific city/canton handling to Address fields.

---

Address for Luxembourg adds a city field and a predefined list of cantons for Luxembourg to the Address module — so addresses in Luxembourg can be entered with proper local administrative divisions (cantons) and city handling, improving address accuracy for Luxembourg-focused sites.

It's an Address-module localization enhancement with no content or access role of its own. Depends on `address`; supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
