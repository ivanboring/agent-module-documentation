Adds Google-related domains to the Content Security Policy rules.

---

CSP Google Supported Domains adds Google-related domains to the CSP policy rules — extending the Content Security Policy (from the `csp` module) with the domains needed by Google services (Analytics, Tag Manager, Fonts, Maps, reCAPTCHA, etc.), so those services keep working under a strict CSP without hand-maintaining the allowlist. Depends on `csp`; supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
