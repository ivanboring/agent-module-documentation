Transliterate Twig adds a Twig filter for transliterating strings (converting accented/non-ASCII characters to ASCII) in templates.

---

Generating an ASCII identifier, a filename, or an anchor from text with accents or non-Latin characters needs transliteration. Transliterate Twig adds a `transliterate` Twig filter for use in theme templates. It is a theming utility with no security surface; the filter is used by template authors (a trusted layer). Confirm the transliteration matches your expected output for the languages involved.
