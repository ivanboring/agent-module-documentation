LocalGov Forms provides additional configuration, styling and components for building forms on LocalGov Drupal government sites, including date, decision-tree and feedback components.

---

Government-service forms have specific needs — accessible date entry, decision trees, feedback forms, GOV.UK-style patterns. LocalGov Forms provides those components and styling for the LocalGov Drupal distribution, with submodules for date, decision tree, a feedback form, an example integration, and LTS support. It is distribution-oriented; on a LocalGov site it supplies council-form building blocks. The security-relevant components are the forms themselves — a feedback form collects submissions (potentially personal data), so apply the usual form data-handling (spam protection, retention, access to submissions). Adopt as part of a LocalGov build.
