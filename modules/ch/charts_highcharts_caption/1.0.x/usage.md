Charts Highcharts Caption adds captions to Highcharts charts in Views.

---

Charts Highcharts Caption provides a Views area plugin that adds a caption to your Highcharts chart — so a chart rendered via the Charts module (Highcharts backend) in a View can have a descriptive caption (accessibility/context), placed in the view's area.

It's a Charts/display enhancement with no content or access role of its own. Depends on `charts` and `charts_highcharts`; supports Drupal 8.8 through 11.
