Chunker splits large content into sections based on inline headings, for paginating or structuring long text.

---

Chunker breaks large amounts of content into sections using the content's own inline headings as
split points — turning a long body into discrete chunks (for pagination, tabs, or a structured reading
experience) without the author manually dividing it. It keys off heading markup in the rendered
content.

Use it for long-form articles or documentation where readers benefit from sectioned navigation derived
automatically from headings. It is a content-display/presentation feature; it restructures how existing
content is shown, not the content or its access. Output is derived from authored markup and rendered
through Drupal's layers.
