Changelogify builds a changelog by collecting site changes into releases.

---

Changelogify automatically collects site changes, groups them into releases, and presents them as a changelog — so a site can maintain a user-facing (or internal) changelog of what changed and when, organized into release entries, useful for communicating updates.

Permissions cover administration (`administer changelogify`), managing releases (`manage changelogify releases`), and viewing (`view changelogify releases`). Depends on core `datetime`, `node`, `options`, and `user`; supports Drupal 10.3+ and 11.
