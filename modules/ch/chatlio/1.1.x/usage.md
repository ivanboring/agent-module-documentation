Chatlio embeds the Chatlio Slack-connected live-chat widget on the site.

---

Chatlio integrates the Chatlio live-chat service — a widget that connects site visitors to your team via Slack — by embedding the Chatlio widget script and configuration into Drupal pages. Visitors can start a chat and your team responds from Slack.

The widget loads Chatlio's third-party JavaScript, so review privacy implications (visitor data goes to Chatlio). It's a front-end integration with no content or access role of its own. Supports Drupal 10.3+ and 11.
