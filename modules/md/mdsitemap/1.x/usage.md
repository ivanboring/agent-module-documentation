Markdown Sitemap generates a dedicated sitemap for LLM crawlers, exposing site content in a markdown/LLM-friendly form (an llms.txt-style resource).

---

As AI crawlers consume web content, sites are adding LLM-oriented resources (llms.txt, markdown sitemaps) to expose content in a form models ingest well. Markdown Sitemap generates such a sitemap for LLM crawlers. It is an SEO/AI-discoverability feature. The consideration is deliberate: it makes site content more available to AI crawlers, which is a policy choice — expose only content you intend AI systems to ingest, and be aware that a markdown content dump is easy for any consumer (not just intended LLMs) to scrape. Confirm the sitemap includes only public, intended-for-AI content.
