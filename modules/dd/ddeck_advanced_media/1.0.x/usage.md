A Plyr player field formatter and a CKEditor 5 media gallery.

---

DDECK Plyr & Advanced Media adds a Plyr player field formatter and a CKEditor 5 gallery using the Media Library and PhotoSwipe — so media fields can render through the Plyr video/audio player and editors can insert an image gallery (backed by the Media Library, viewed with PhotoSwipe) into CKEditor 5. Depends on core `media`, `media_library`, `ckeditor5`, and `image`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
