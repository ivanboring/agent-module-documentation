Autocomplete Field Match allows matching fields other than title with autocomplete.

---

Autocomplete Field Match lets **autocomplete match on fields other than the entity title** — so an
entity-reference autocomplete can suggest/match by another field (SKU, email, etc.) instead of only the label.
It depends on core Field and Field UI.

Use it to autocomplete-reference by a non-title field. It is a content-editing/field feature; the suggested
entities follow normal entity access (the autocomplete respects access) and it has no access-control role.
Configure which field the autocomplete matches.
