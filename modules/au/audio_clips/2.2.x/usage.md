Audio Clips adds audio-clip entities/API for MP3 and WAV audio.

---

Audio Clips provides an Audio Clips API — a framework for managing audio clips (MP3, WAV) as configurable types/entities, so a site can store, organize and serve audio clips (sound effects, snippets, recordings) through a structured API.

Administration of clip types is gated by `administer audio clip types`. It's a media/API module; supports Drupal 10.1+ and 11.
