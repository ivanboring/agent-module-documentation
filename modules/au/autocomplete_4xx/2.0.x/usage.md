Autocomplete 4xx adds an autocomplete field for selecting content for 4xx error pages.

---

Autocomplete 4xx provides an autocomplete field type for 4xx pages — letting site builders configure custom content/pages for HTTP 4xx errors (403, 404) by selecting existing content via an autocomplete widget, streamlining error-page configuration.

It's a field/site-building utility with no content or access role of its own. Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
