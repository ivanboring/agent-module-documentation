Autocomplete Extras adds configurable per-field settings to autocomplete widgets.

---

Autocomplete Extras enhances Drupal's autocomplete widget with per-instance settings — giving site builders more control over how autocomplete fields behave (min characters, result limits, matching) on a per-field basis, beyond the default fixed behavior.

It's a field-widget enhancement with no content or access role of its own. Supports Drupal 10.3+ and 11.
