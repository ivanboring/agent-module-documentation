Author Field adds field types for researcher/author data keyed to an ORCID identifier.

---

Author Field provides field types for storing author information associated with an ORCID identifier — the standard persistent digital identifier for researchers. It's aimed at academic/research sites that need to attach structured, ORCID-linked author metadata to content.

Administration is gated by `administer author_field`. It's a field-provider module with no front-end access role; render output should be escaped normally. Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
