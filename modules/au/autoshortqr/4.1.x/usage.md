AutoShortQR provides a self referencing QR code using drupal/barcode.

---

AutoShortQR **auto-generates a self-referencing QR code** for pages — producing a QR code (via the
drupal/barcode library) that encodes the current page's URL, so visitors can scan to open/share it. It depends
on the Barcodes module.

Use it to add scan-to-open QR codes to pages. It is a content-display/user-engagement feature; the QR encodes a
public page URL and it has no content or access role. Configure the QR code display.
