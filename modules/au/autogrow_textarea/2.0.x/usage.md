Autogrow Textarea grows/shrinks textareas to fit their content as the user types.

---

Autogrow Textarea is a UI enhancement that automatically resizes textarea fields to fit their content as the user types, removing the need to scroll within a small box. It applies to textareas in forms across the site.

It's a front-end convenience with no content or access role and no permissions. Supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.

---

- Auto-resize textareas to content.
- Grow the textarea as the user types.
- Remove in-box scrolling.
- Enhance form UX.
- Apply across site forms.
- Require no configuration.
- Carry no permissions.
- Carry no content/access role.
- Support Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
- Improve editing comfort.
- Work on any textarea.
- Adjust height dynamically.
- Keep the enhancement lightweight.
- Act as a UI convenience.
- Support long-text entry.

- Improve accessibility of large text.
