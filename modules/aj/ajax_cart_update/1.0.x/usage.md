AJAX Cart Update enhances the Commerce cart form with AJAX to update quantities and order totals dynamically.

---

AJAX Cart Update enhances the Drupal Commerce cart form with AJAX — updating line-item quantities and
recalculating order totals dynamically (without a full page reload), for a smoother cart experience. It
depends on core Views (the cart form is a View).

Use it to make the Commerce cart update in place. It is an e-commerce/UX feature affecting the cart form's
behaviour; the cart operations still go through Commerce's normal handling and access, and it has no
access-control role. Enable it on the cart.
