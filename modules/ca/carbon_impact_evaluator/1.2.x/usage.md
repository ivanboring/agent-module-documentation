Carbon Impact Evaluator estimates the environmental footprint of pages.

---

Carbon impact evaluator estimates the carbon/environmental impact of a Drupal site's pages — measuring page weight and estimating the CO2/energy footprint of serving them, so site owners can assess and reduce the environmental impact of their site (sustainable web design).

It's a performance/sustainability analysis tool with no content or access role of its own. Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
