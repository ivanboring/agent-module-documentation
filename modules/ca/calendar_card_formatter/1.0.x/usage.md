Calendar Card Formatter renders Date fields as visual calendar cards.

---

Calendar Card Formatter displays a calendar card style for Date fields — rendering a date as a visual 'calendar page' card (month + day) instead of plain text, a common design element for event listings and date displays.

It's a field formatter with no content or access role of its own. Depends on core `datetime`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
