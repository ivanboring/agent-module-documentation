CAS User Ban prevents account creation for banned CAS usernames.

---

CAS User Ban **blocks account creation for banned CAS usernames** — maintaining a list of CAS usernames that
are prevented from having (or creating) a Drupal account when authenticating via CAS. It depends on the CAS module.

Use it to deny specific CAS identities. It is an access/authentication-control feature that complements CAS: it
stops the auto-provisioning of Drupal accounts for denied CAS usernames (a targeted deny-list on top of CAS
single-sign-on). Gate its management to trusted admins and keep the ban list accurate. It has no broader
access-control role. Configure the CAS username ban list.
