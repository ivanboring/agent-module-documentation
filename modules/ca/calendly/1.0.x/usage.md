Calendly provides an easy and simple way to embed Calendly scheduling pages on Drupal.

---

Calendly embeds **Calendly scheduling pages** into Drupal — so visitors can book meetings/appointments
through Calendly's inline or popup widget without leaving the site. You configure your Calendly URL and it
renders the embed, in the Calendly package.

Use it to add Calendly booking to pages. It is a third-party integration feature — the scheduling flow (and
any data the visitor enters) runs on **Calendly's** side, and the module loads **Calendly's third-party
JavaScript** (trust the vendor; runs in your origin) and processes visitor scheduling data (a privacy/consent
consideration). It has no access-control role. Configure the Calendly URL and embed.
