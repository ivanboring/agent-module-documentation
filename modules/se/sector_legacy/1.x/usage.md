Sector Legacy provides support for older features and packages of the Sector distribution/theme, with admin-UI-toggle, blocks and utils submodules.

---

Sector is a Drupal distribution/theme; Sector Legacy provides backward-compatibility support for its older features, with `admin_ui_toggle`, `sector_blocks` and `sector_utils` submodules. It is distribution compatibility infrastructure. No unusual security surface of its own; it is for Sector sites carrying legacy features forward. Adopt only on a Sector site needing the legacy support.
