Seeds Pollination provides miscellaneous enhancements for the Seeds distribution, tying its components together.

---

Seeds Pollination is a glue module for the Seeds distribution — a collection of miscellaneous enhancements and integrations that make the distribution's components work well together. Like other distribution glue, it is meant for Seeds sites and carries the distribution's assumptions; on an unrelated site it is unlikely to be useful on its own. It has no notable security surface of its own.
