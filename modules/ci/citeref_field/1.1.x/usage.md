Citation Reference Field adds field types for citation/reference records keyed to DOI/Handle/ARK or custom methods.

---

Citation Reference Field (Citeref) provides field types for storing citation and reference information — records identified via DOI, Handle, or ARK registries, or specified through URL/URN/other methods. It's aimed at academic, library and research sites that need structured, resolvable reference metadata on content.

Administration is gated by `administer citeref_field`. It's a field provider with no front-end access role; render output is escaped normally. Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
