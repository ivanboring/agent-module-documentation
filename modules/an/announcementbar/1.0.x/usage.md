Announcement Bar shows a site-wide announcement banner block.

---

Announcement Bar displays a configurable announcement bar — a site-wide banner (via a block) for showing important messages, promotions, or notices to visitors, with configurable text/styling and placement.

It's a content-display/block feature with no content or access role of its own. Depends on core `block`; supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
