Analyze AI Brand Voice analyzes content for brand-voice consistency using AI, part of the Analyze module ecosystem.

---

Keeping a consistent brand voice across content is hard to check manually. Analyze AI Brand Voice uses AI to assess content against a brand voice, as an Analyze submodule. It sends content to a configured AI provider, so the provider key is a credential (keep out of plain config) and analysed content leaves your infrastructure (governance decision for confidential content). The output is advisory. Watch API cost.
