Analyze AI Sentiments provides AI-powered sentiment analysis of content, part of the Analyze module ecosystem.

---

Understanding the sentiment of content or feedback benefits from AI. Analyze AI Sentiments assesses sentiment via a configured AI provider, as an Analyze submodule. It sends content to the AI provider, so the provider key is a credential (keep out of plain config) and analysed content leaves your infrastructure (governance for confidential content).
