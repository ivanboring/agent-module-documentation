Announce Autosubmit adds an ARIA-live announcement for auto-submitting forms.

---

A11y Announce Form Auto-Submit shows an accessible (ARIA-live) announcement when a form is auto-submitted and keeps keyboard focus in place — so screen-reader and keyboard users are informed when a form (e.g. an exposed filter) auto-submits and don't lose their place, improving accessibility of AJAX/auto-submit forms.

It's an accessibility enhancement with no content or access role of its own. Supports Drupal 8 through 11.
