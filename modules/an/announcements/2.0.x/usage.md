Announcements manages site announcement display.

---

Announcements manages **site announcements** — configurable notices shown to visitors with display
conditions (via `condition_field`) and cookie-based dismissal (via `js_cookie`), useful for banners, alerts or
promotions that appear on chosen pages and can be dismissed. It provides its own permissions, in the
Announcements package.

Use it to show conditional, dismissible announcements. It is a user-engagement feature; announcement content
is admin-authored (rendered markup — keep it trusted) and it sets a dismissal cookie, and it has no
access-control role beyond its permission. Configure the announcements and conditions.
