Integrates Discourse comments into Drupal content.

---

Discourse comments (+) provides Discourse comments integration with Drupal — embedding a Discourse forum topic's comments on Drupal content (via a field), so discussion happens in Discourse while displaying on the Drupal page, offloading community discussion to Discourse. Depends on core `field`; supports Drupal 10.3+ and 11.
