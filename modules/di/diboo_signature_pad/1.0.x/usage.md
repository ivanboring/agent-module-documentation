A signature-pad drawing widget for the Diboo ecosystem.

---

Diboo signature pad allows a signature pad to be used as a drawing tool — providing a form widget where users draw a signature (or freehand drawing) on a canvas, captured for storage, part of the Diboo ecosystem. Depends on `diboo_core` and `signature_pad`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
