Image Scale and Fill provides a custom image-style effect that scales an image and fills to target dimensions.

---

Fitting varied images to a fixed slot — a card, a thumbnail — needs scaling with fill to avoid letterboxing or distortion. Image Scale and Fill is an image-style effect that scales and fills to the target size. It is an image-processing effect with no security surface. Confirm the fill behaviour (crop vs pad) suits your images.
