Add Fediverse (Mastodon etc.) social link fields to entities.

---

Fediverse Social Link Field adds fediverse link fields to your entities — extending the Social Link Field module with field types/widgets for Fediverse networks (Mastodon, PeerTube, etc.), so profiles/content can store and display links to fediverse accounts. Depends on `social_link_field`; supports Drupal 10.3+ and 11.
