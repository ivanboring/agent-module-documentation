Feeds Conditional Tamper adds Tamper plugins to conditionally skip items/values during Feeds imports.

---

Feeds Conditional Tamper provides Feeds Tamper plugins that conditionally skip an entire feed item, or a specific field value, based on a condition evaluated against a source field — giving finer control over which imported data is kept during a Feeds import.

It's a Feeds/Tamper extension with no content or access role of its own. Depends on `tamper` and `feeds_tamper`; supports Drupal 10.2+ and 11.
