Custom Page Title allows setting a specific custom page title.

---

Custom Page Title lets you override/set a specific page title (the `<title>` / H1) for pages —
customizing what title is shown, useful for SEO or presentation where the default title isn't ideal. It is
configured at `custom_page_title.custom_page_title_settings_form`.

Use it to control page titles. It is an SEO/display feature affecting the page title; it does not change
content or access and has no access-control role. Configure the custom titles.
