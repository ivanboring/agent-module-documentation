A field type/widget/formatter to generate a Table of Contents from a CKEditor field.

---

Custom TOC adds a TOC-overrides field type, widget, and formatter to generate and render a Table of Contents from a CKEditor source field, including TOC text updates — so editors get an automatically-built, editable table of contents for long rich-text content, with the ability to override individual TOC entry text. Depends on `toc_api`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
