Editorial access manager assigns individual users the right to edit or translate a specific content entity — per-item editorial delegation, including per language.

---

Drupal's editing permissions are per bundle: a role may edit all articles or none. Editorial reality is frequently per item — this page belongs to the finance team, that one to a named author, this translation is assigned to a particular translator — and the standard answers are heavyweight (Group, with a membership model) or coarse (a role per team). This module takes the assignment approach: an assignment form at `/editorial-access-manager/editorial-assignment/{entity_type_id}/{entity_id}/{langcode}` — note the **langcode in the path**, which is the distinctive part, since a translation can be assigned independently of the source — plus `EditorialAccessManager`, an event subscriber and a dedicated `EditorialAccessManagerTranslationHandler`. Permissions are partly generated by `EditorialAccessManagerPermissions::permissions()` alongside declared ones including `assign entity edition` and `reassign assigned entities`, the latter covering the handover case when someone leaves. Dependencies are core `node` and `content_translation`, with a wide range of `^8 || ^9 || ^10 || ^11`. As with any access module, verify behaviour in Views, JSON:API and search rather than only on the entity form — that is where per-item access most often fails to apply.
