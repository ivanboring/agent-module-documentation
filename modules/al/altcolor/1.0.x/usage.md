Alternative Color provides theme color customization as a modern Color-module alternative.

---

Alternative Color (altcolor) is a modern alternative to the legacy Drupal Color module — allowing administrators to customize a theme's color scheme through the UI, replacing the deprecated/removed core Color module functionality on newer Drupal versions.

It's a theming utility with no content or access role of its own. Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
