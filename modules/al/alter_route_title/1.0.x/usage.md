Alter Route Title helps you alter route titles.

---

Alter Route Title lets you **alter the page title of specific routes** — overriding the title shown on
chosen routes (admin or front-end pages) without custom code, for branding/clarity. It is in the Utility
package.

Use it to customize page titles per route. It is a site-building/UX feature affecting the title; titles are
admin-configured and it has no content or access role. Configure the route-title overrides.
