Breakpoint Field lets editors pick a breakpoint group and select from its breakpoints on content.

---

Breakpoint Field defines a field type where an editor selects a breakpoint group and then chooses among the breakpoints it defines, storing that selection on content. This lets responsive behaviour or per-breakpoint configuration be driven by editorial choices rather than hard-coded theme logic.

It's a field-provider/site-builder tool; render output is escaped normally and it has no access role of its own. Depends on core `field` and `field_ui`; supports Drupal 10, 11, and 12.
