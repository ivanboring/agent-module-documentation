Brevo Webform Handler is a Webform handler that sends submissions to Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) as contacts, for marketing-list integration.

---

When a Webform is a signup or lead form, the submission often needs to become a contact in a marketing platform. Brevo Webform Handler sends submissions to Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) as contacts. It needs a Brevo API key (a credential to keep out of plain config), and it forwards personal data — the submission — to an external marketing service, so the form's privacy posture (disclosure, consent) extends to Brevo. Configure the field mapping and capture consent before submissions become marketing contacts.
