Push Framework Email provides an email push-notification channel for the Push Framework, sending notifications via email.

---

The Push Framework abstracts sending notifications over channels; Push Framework Email is the email channel. It sends framework notifications as emails. It is notification infrastructure. No unusual security surface beyond standard email (notification content in emails, sent to the configured recipients). Confirm notification content and recipients are appropriate, and that email sending is configured (a mail transport).
