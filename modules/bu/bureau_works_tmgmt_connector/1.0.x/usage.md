Bureau Works TMGMT Connector a TMGMT translator connector for the Bureau Works translation platform.

---

Bureau Works TMGMT Connector **provides a TMGMT translator for the Bureau Works** translation platform —
sending content to Bureau Works for translation and receiving results, via TMGMT, with signed requests. It is in
the Translation Management package.

Use it to translate content with Bureau Works. It is a multilingual/integration feature. Security/data handling:
it **sends content to the external Bureau Works platform** (data egress — confirm acceptable, including drafts/
sensitive text) and authenticates with **API credentials** (store as secrets — env/Key — over HTTPS; requests are
signed). It has no access-control role. Configure the Bureau Works credentials.
