Business Identity manages business identity information (name, logo, contact, legal details) centrally.

---

Business Identity **manages business identity information centrally** — storing the organization's name,
logo, contact and legal details in one place for reuse across the site (footers, metadata, structured data). It
depends on core System and Config, provides its own permissions, in the Business package.

Use it to centralize business identity data. It is a site-building/administration feature; the data is
admin-managed config and it has no access-control role beyond its permission. Configure the business identity.
