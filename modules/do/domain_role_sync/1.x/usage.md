Sync users to domains when they get a role, and vice versa.

---

Domain role synchronization allows users to be assigned to domains when they get a role and vice versa — keeping Domain access memberships and Drupal roles in sync, so granting a role automatically adds the user to the mapped domain(s) and vice versa, easing multi-site/domain access administration. Depends on `domain`; supports Drupal 9 through 12.
