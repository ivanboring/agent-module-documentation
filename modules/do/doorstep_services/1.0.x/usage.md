A platform for customers to register for doorstep services.

---

Doorstep Services provides a platform for customers to register for doorstep services — letting a site offer and take bookings/registrations for at-home services (a service listing plus a customer registration flow), useful for service-business sites. Depends on core `node`, `user`, and `views`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
