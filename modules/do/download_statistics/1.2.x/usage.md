Download Statistics logs statistics about file downloads on the site, tracking how often files are downloaded.

---

Download Statistics logs file-download activity — recording how often files (attached to nodes/
fields) are downloaded, so site owners can see which documents/assets are popular. It depends on core
Node, Field and File, is configured at `download_statistics.settings`, and provides its own
permissions.

Use it to measure engagement with downloadable content (PDFs, resources, media). It records download
events and surfaces counts/reports. As with any analytics that logs requests, be mindful of what is
stored per download (avoid logging unnecessary personal data, and consider privacy/retention). It is an
administration/reporting feature; access to the statistics is governed by its permissions, and it does
not change content access.
