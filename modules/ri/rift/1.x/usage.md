RIFT provides services, field formatters and Twig helpers (with a starter kit and UI submodule) — a front-end toolkit for building interfaces.

---

RIFT is a front-end toolkit: services, field formatters and Twig helpers for building interfaces, with `rift_starter_kit` and `rift_ui` submodules. It is developer/theming infrastructure; what it does depends on how its helpers are used. As a toolkit it has no fixed security surface of its own — the Twig helpers render in the theme layer (trusted), and field formatters display access-controlled field data. Review how a given RIFT-built interface uses the toolkit.
