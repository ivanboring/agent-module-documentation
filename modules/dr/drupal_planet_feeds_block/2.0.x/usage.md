Drupal Planet Feeds Block displays recent Drupal Planet aggregator posts in a block.

---

Drupal Planet Feeds Block provides a block that displays the latest entries from the Drupal Planet feed — the aggregated blog feed of the Drupal community — so a site can surface recent community news/blog posts in a sidebar or region.

It fetches an external feed; be mindful of caching and feed availability. Depends on core `views`; supports Drupal 8.8+, 9, 10, and 11.
