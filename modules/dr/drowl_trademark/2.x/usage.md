Dynamically appends a registered-trademark symbol to defined texts via JS.

---

DROWL Trademark appends a registered trademark symbol to defined texts via JavaScript dynamically — so configured brand/product names automatically get a ® (or similar) superscript appended in the rendered page, without editing the content, keeping trademark marking consistent. It's a frontend/JS display tweak. Supports Drupal 8.9 through 11.
