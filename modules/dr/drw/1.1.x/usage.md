DRW provides a date range validation widget with configuration.

---

DRW (Date Range Widget) provides a **date-range field widget with configurable validation** — enforcing
rules on a date-range field such as start-before-end, minimum/maximum span, or allowed ranges, at input time. It
is in the Field package.

Use it to validate date-range input. It is a content-editing/forms feature affecting the widget and validation;
it has no content or access role. Configure the validation on the date-range field.
