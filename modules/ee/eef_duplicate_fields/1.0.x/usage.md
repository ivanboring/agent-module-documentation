Entity Extra Field plugins for duplicating fields with custom formatters.

---

EEF - Duplicate Fields provides Entity Extra Field plugins for duplicating fields with custom formatters and rendering referenced-entity fields — so on an entity's display you can add extra computed fields that mirror (duplicate) another field or pull in fields from a referenced entity, with your choice of formatter. Depends on `entity_extra_field`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
