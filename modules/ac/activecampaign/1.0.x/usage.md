ActiveCampaign integrates the ActiveCampaign marketing-automation and CRM platform, with dashboard and webform submodules.

---

ActiveCampaign integrates the ActiveCampaign marketing-automation/CRM platform with Drupal — syncing
contacts and enabling marketing-automation features, with `activecampaign_dashboard` and
`activecampaign_webform` submodules (dashboard view and webform integration). It is in the Automated
marketing package.

Use it to connect Drupal to ActiveCampaign for contact syncing and marketing automation. The
security/privacy-relevant points: store the ActiveCampaign API credentials as secrets, and contact data
(emails, names — personal data) is transmitted to ActiveCampaign — obtain appropriate consent and disclose
the data sharing (GDPR). It is an integration/marketing feature with no access-control role. Configure the
ActiveCampaign connection.
