Academic Marksheet records student marks and produces result sheets.

---

Academic Marksheet is a module to manage student marks and generate results — recording marks per student/subject and producing marksheets/result outputs, aimed at schools/academic sites that need basic grade management inside Drupal.

Permissions cover administration (`administer marksheet`), assigning marks (`assign marks`), and self-service results (`view own results`) — marks are personal data, so restrict assign/admin to staff. Depends on core `views`, `field`, `user`, and `taxonomy`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
