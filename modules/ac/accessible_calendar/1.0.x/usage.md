Accessible Calendar provides accessible month and week calendars.

---

Accessible Calendar **provides accessible month and week calendar displays** — rendering event/date content
as calendars built for keyboard and screen-reader accessibility (ARIA, focus management). It depends on core
Views.

Use it to show accessible calendars. It is a content-display/accessibility feature; calendar content comes from
access-respecting Views and it has no access-control role. Configure the calendar display.
