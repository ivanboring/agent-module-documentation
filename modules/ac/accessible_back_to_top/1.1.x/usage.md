Accessible Back To Top adds an accessible back to top button.

---

Accessible Back To Top adds a "back to top" button to pages — an accessibility-focused scroll-to-top
control (keyboard-operable, screen-reader friendly) that appears as the visitor scrolls. It is in the Custom
package.

Use it to add an accessible back-to-top button. It is a content-display/accessibility feature affecting
navigation UX; it has no content or access role. Enable and configure the button.
