Booking Scheduler adds bookings with scheduling, availability tracking and moderation.

---

Booking Scheduler provides generic booking functionality on top of Drupal content: it models bookable items, tracks availability, and schedules bookings using content moderation and the Scheduler module. It suits reservations, appointments, and resource-booking scenarios without a full commerce stack.

It builds on many core modules (`content_moderation`, `datetime`, `field`, `menu_ui`, `node`, `options`, `path`, `taxonomy`, `text`, `workflows`) plus the `scheduler` contrib module. A content-modelling/booking tool; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
