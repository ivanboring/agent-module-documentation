BOL (Bill of Lading) produces a bill of lading (inventory/shipping document) from content.

---

BOL **produces a bill of lading** — generating an inventory/shipping document (the bill of lading listing
goods) from site content/data, for logistics workflows. It is in the Custom package.

Use it to generate bills of lading. It is a content/business feature. Data-handling note: a bill of lading
contains **business/shipment data** (shipper/consignee details, goods) — handle/protect the generated documents
per your data policy. It has no access-control role. Configure and generate the BOL.
