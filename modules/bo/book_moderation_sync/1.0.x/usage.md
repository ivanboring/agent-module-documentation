Book Moderation Sync keeps a book's child pages' moderation states in sync with the parent.

---

Book Moderation Sync synchronizes moderation states between books and their children — so when a book page's content-moderation state changes (e.g. published/archived), its child pages' states are synchronized, keeping an entire book's workflow status consistent.

It builds on core Content Moderation and Book. It's an editorial-workflow enhancement with no content or access role of its own. Supports Drupal 10.3+ and 11.
