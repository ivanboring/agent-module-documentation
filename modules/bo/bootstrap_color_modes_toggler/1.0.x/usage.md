Bootstrap Theme Toggler provides a block to switch a Bootstrap theme between light and dark modes.

---

Bootstrap Theme Toggler adds a switch block that lets visitors toggle a Bootstrap-based theme between light and dark color modes, persisting the choice. It's aimed at Bootstrap 5 themes that support color modes and want a ready-made toggle UI.

It's a theming enhancement with no content or access role and no permissions of its own. Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
