Video Embed TikTok adds TikTok as a provider for Video Embed Field, so a TikTok URL renders as an embedded player.

---

Organisations publish to TikTok because that is where a particular audience is, and then want the same video on their own site — in a news story, on a campaign page, in a case study — because the site is where the content is permanent, findable and under the organisation's control while the platform is neither. The embed is the bridge. Version **3.0.0-beta1** — a **beta** — on core `^10.3 || ^11`, requiring `video_embed_field`, with everything except URL handling and the embed markup coming from the parent module. Three things belong in any conversation about embedding this particular platform, and they are heavier than for YouTube or Vimeo. **The consent question is sharper**: TikTok's embed loads tracking from a platform whose data handling has been the subject of regulatory action in the EU, the UK and the US, and several public bodies restrict or prohibit its use on official devices and sites — so for a government or health organisation this is a policy question before it is a technical one, and the answer may be that the embed is not permitted even where the account is. **Short-form video is rarely captioned** and platform auto-captions are not a substitute, so a site republishing this content carries the accessibility obligation the platform does not enforce. And **the content is not the organisation's to keep**: an embed goes blank when the video is removed, the account is suspended or the platform changes its embed rules, so anything that matters should exist as a hosted copy with the embed as a convenience rather than the other way round.
