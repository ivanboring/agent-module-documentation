AI Validations adds AI support to the Field Validation module.

---

AI Validations **adds AI to Field Validation** — providing AI-powered validation rules for the Field
Validation module (e.g. validating field content against AI-evaluated criteria). It depends on the AI, Field
Validation and core Image modules.

Use it for AI-assisted field validation. It is an AI/forms feature. Security/data handling: it **sends field
content to the configured AI provider** (egress) to evaluate validation, relying on the AI module for credentials
(secrets). It has no access-control role. Configure the AI validation rules.
