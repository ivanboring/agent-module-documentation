AIA generates Drupal configuration (content types, fields, views, menus) from AI prompts.

---

AI Assistant (AIA) is an AI-powered Drupal configuration generator — it creates content types, fields, taxonomy, block content, views and menu links from AI prompts, so site builders can scaffold a site's structure by describing it in natural language rather than clicking through the UI.

AI operations run through the configured provider (cost) and the generation creates configuration/structure on the site; administration is gated by `administer aia` — keep it to trusted site builders/admins. Depends on core `node`, `field`, `taxonomy`, `block_content`, `views`, `menu_link_content`, and `ai`; supports Drupal 10.2+ and 11.
