AI Eval provides a framework to evaluate AI agents with graders and YAML datasets.

---

AI Eval is an evaluation framework for AI agents — providing pluggable graders, YAML-defined datasets, and judge validation so teams can systematically test/score AI agent behavior against expected outcomes, with annotatable results.

Evaluation runs through the configured AI provider (cost). Permissions cover operating (`operate ai eval`), administration (`administer ai eval`), annotating results, and validating judges. Depends on `ai`, core `options`, and `file`; requires Drupal 11.3+.
