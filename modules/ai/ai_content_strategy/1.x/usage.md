AI Content Strategy provides AI-powered content-strategy recommendations based on the site's content.

---

Planning what content to create — gaps, topics, structure — is where AI can advise. AI Content Strategy analyses the site's content and returns strategy recommendations via a configured AI provider. It builds on the AI module. The considerations are the standard AI ones: the provider key is a credential (keep out of plain config), and content analysed is sent to the AI provider (a governance decision for confidential content). The output is advisory — recommendations a human acts on.
