AI Content Lifecycle uses AI to detect and mark outdated content.

---

AI Content Lifecycle **manages content freshness with AI** — using AI to assess content and automatically mark
outdated content for review/refresh, for content-governance. It depends on the AI module and provides its own
permissions.

Use it to flag stale content with AI. It is an AI/content-governance feature. Security/data handling: it **sends
content to the configured AI provider** (egress — content can be unpublished/sensitive; confirm acceptable) and
relies on the AI module for provider credentials (secrets). It has no access-control role beyond its permission.
