AI Metatag Generator auto-writes SEO meta tags for nodes via AI.

---

AI Metatag Generator generates meta tags (title, description, etc.) for nodes using AI — producing SEO meta content automatically from a node's body via the AI module and the Metatag module, so editors get AI-suggested metadata without writing it manually.

Generation runs through the configured AI provider (cost). Permissions cover administration (`administer ai metatag generator`) and use (`use ai metatag generator`). Depends on `ai` and `metatag`; supports Drupal 8 through 11.
