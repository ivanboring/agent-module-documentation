AI CIJO detects user intent with AI and orchestrates blocks/content along journeys.

---

AI Content Intent & Journey Orchestrator (AI CIJO) provides AI-driven intent detection and journey-based orchestration of blocks — using AI to infer a visitor's intent and then dynamically arrange/show blocks and content along a configured journey, for personalized, intent-aware experiences.

AI operations run through the configured provider (cost). Administration is gated by `administer ai cijo`. Depends on core `block` and `views`; requires Drupal 11.
