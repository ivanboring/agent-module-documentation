---

AI Audio Field adds an audio field enriched by AI (e.g. AI-generated audio/transcription). It builds on Drupal's AI module and depends on `ai`, `file`, in the AI Tools package.

Use it for AI-assisted audio. It is an AI/integration feature. Security/data handling: it **sends
audio/text to the configured AI provider** (external egress — confirm acceptable for the content), the provider **API key** is stored via the AI module's Key config (secret),
over HTTPS. It has no access-control role. Configure the AI
provider.
