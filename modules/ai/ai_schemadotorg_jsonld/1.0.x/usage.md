AI Schema.org JSON-LD uses AI Automators to generate and attach Schema.org JSON-LD.

---

AI Schema.org JSON-LD **uses AI (via AI Automators) to generate Schema.org JSON-LD** structured data and
attach it to content — so pages get rich, schema.org-marked-up metadata for search engines without hand-writing
it. It depends on AI Automators, JSON Field and Field Widget Actions, with breadcrumb and log submodules, in
the AI package (core 11.3+).

Use it to auto-generate structured data with AI. It is an AI/SEO feature. Security/data handling: it **sends
content to the configured AI provider** to generate the JSON-LD (external data egress if the provider is
cloud-based — confirm acceptable), AI **credentials** are handled via the AI module (store as secrets), and the
generated JSON-LD is emitted into the page — review AI output before relying on it. It has no access-control
role. Configure the AI automator and schema mapping.
