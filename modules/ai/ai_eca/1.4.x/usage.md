AI ECA integration (deprecated) exposes AI operations to the ECA no-code automation system.

---

AI ECA integration is a submodule of the AI project that integrates AI operations with ECA (Event-Condition-Action) — providing ECA actions/conditions so no-code ECA models can invoke AI. NOTE: it is **deprecated and being removed in AI 2.0.0**, so new sites should not rely on it.

AI operations run through the configured provider (cost). It's part of the `ai` project; supports Drupal 10.3+ and 11.
