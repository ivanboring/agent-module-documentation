AI Dropsolid a Dropsolid-specific AI bundle providing preconfigured AI provider settings. It builds on Drupal's AI module and depends on `ai_provider_dropsolidai`, in the AI package.

Use it for AI-assisted llm / ai provider. It is an AI/integration feature. Security/data handling: it **sends
prompts/content to the configured AI provider** (external egress — confirm acceptable for the content), the provider **API key** is stored via the AI module's Key config (secret),
over HTTPS. It has no access-control role. Configure the AI
provider.
