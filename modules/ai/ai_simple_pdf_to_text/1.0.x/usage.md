AI Simple PDF to Text offers automators and agents to convert PDF to text using the AI module.

---

AI Simple PDF to Text provides automators/agents for the AI module that convert PDF documents to text —
extracting the textual content of PDFs (for indexing, summarizing, or further AI processing) via the AI
framework. It is in the AI Tools package.

Use it to turn PDFs into text within AI workflows. Security/data note: converting a PDF typically sends its
content to the configured AI provider (an external service) — a **data-handling consideration for sensitive/
confidential PDFs** (don't send documents you can't share externally), and store any provider API key as a
secret. It has no access-control role. Configure the automator and AI provider.
