---

AI RAG Assistant provides an AI chatbot using Retrieval-Augmented Generation over site content. It builds on Drupal's AI module and depends on `node`, in the AI package.

Use it for AI-assisted chatbots. It is an AI/integration feature. Security/data handling: it **sends
content/queries to the configured AI provider** (external egress — confirm acceptable for the content), the provider **API key** is stored via the AI module's Key config (secret),
over HTTPS. It has no access-control role beyond its permission. Configure the AI
provider.
