AI Webform Guard protects webforms from spam using AI.

---

AI Webform Guard **protects Webforms from spam using AI** — running submissions through an AI model to
classify/block spammy content before it's stored, as an intelligent alternative/complement to CAPTCHA/Honeypot.
It depends on the AI and Webform modules, in the Spam Protection package.

Use it for AI-based form spam protection. It is a spam-protection/AI feature. Security/data handling: submission
content is **sent to the configured AI provider** to classify (external egress — confirm acceptable, as
submissions may contain PII; key via AI/Key secret), and (as with any AI classifier) treat it as one layer —
combine with server-side anti-spam and don't rely solely on it. It has no access-control role. Configure the AI
provider and guard.
