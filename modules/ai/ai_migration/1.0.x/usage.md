AI Migration uses AI to assist building migrations from JSON:API/schema sources.

---

AI Migration leverages AI to make migration of content easier — using AI together with JSON:API, Migrate, and schema (schemata) tooling to help generate migration mappings/configuration, reducing the manual effort of modeling and migrating content into Drupal.

AI operations run through the configured provider (cost). Depends on `ai`, core `jsonapi`, `migrate`, `migrate_plus`, `serialization`, and the `schemata`/`schemata_json_schema` modules; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
