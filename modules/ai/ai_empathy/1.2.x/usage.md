AI Empathy Evaluation scores AI responses on empathy/ethics using dilemma scenarios.

---

AI Empathy Evaluation evaluates and improves AI empathetic decision-making using ethical dilemmas — running AI responses against scenario datasets to assess empathy/ethical reasoning, so teams can measure and refine how their AI features handle sensitive/ethical situations.

Evaluation runs through the configured AI provider (cost). Permissions cover administration, running evaluations, and viewing/rating results. Depends on `ai`; supports Drupal 10.4+, 11, and 12.
