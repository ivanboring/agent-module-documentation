AI Comment Guard uses AI to detect and moderate abusive/offensive comments.

---

AI Comment Guard detects and handles abusive, offensive, or harmful language in Drupal comments using AI — screening submitted comments through an AI moderation step and acting on flagged content (block, hold, sanitize), reducing toxic comments on sites with open commenting.

Screening sends comment text to the configured AI provider (cost + data egress). Permissions cover administration (`administer comment sanitizer`) and a `bypass comment sanitizer` permission for trusted roles. Depends on core `comment` and `system`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
