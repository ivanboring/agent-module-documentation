AI Content Creator generates high-quality content with AI.

---

AI Content Creator **generates content with AI** — drafting articles/text from prompts via an AI model, to
speed up content production. It is in the Content package.

Use it to AI-draft content. It is an AI/content-editing feature. Security/data handling: it **sends prompts/
content to the configured AI provider** (external egress — confirm acceptable; the provider API key is stored via
the AI module's Key config, secret), and AI output should be **reviewed/edited** by a human before publishing
(accuracy, licensing, hallucination). It has no access-control role. Configure the AI provider and generate
content.
