AI Content Advisor analyzes your content and returns recommendations.

---

AI Content Advisor **analyzes content with AI and returns recommendations** — running a piece of content
through an AI model to suggest improvements (readability, SEO, tone, completeness) for editors. It depends on
the AI module, provides its own permissions, in the AI package.

Use it to get AI writing/SEO advice on content. It is an AI/content-editing feature. Security/data handling: it
**sends content to the configured AI provider** (external egress — confirm acceptable; key via AI/Key secret),
and recommendations are advisory (editors decide). It has no access-control role beyond its permission. Configure
the AI provider and enable the advisor.
