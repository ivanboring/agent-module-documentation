AI Evaluations runs evaluations on the AI Chatbot.

---

AI Evaluations **runs evaluations on AI Chatbot / AI Assistant responses** — testing/scoring an AI
assistant's answers (against expected outputs or criteria) to measure quality/accuracy over time. It depends on
core Views, AI Assistant API and AI Chatbot, provides its own permissions, in the AI Tools package.

Use it to evaluate AI assistant quality. It is an AI/developer feature. Security/data handling: evaluations
**send test prompts/content to the AI provider** (external egress) and store results — gate its permission to
trusted developers/admins, and keep evaluation data (which may include sample content) handled appropriately. It
has no access-control role beyond its permission. Configure and run evaluations.
