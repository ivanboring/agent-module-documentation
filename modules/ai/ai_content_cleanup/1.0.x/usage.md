AI Content Cleanup detects and fixes issues in migrated/legacy content with AI assistance.

---

AI Content Cleanup provides AI-assisted cleanup workflows for migrated and legacy content — detecting issues (broken markup, formatting, inconsistencies) and helping fix them via AI, so content imported from legacy systems can be normalized more efficiently.

AI operations run through the configured provider (cost). Permissions cover access (`access ai content cleanup`) and administration (`administer ai content cleanup`). Depends on core `node` and `text`; supports Drupal 10.3+ and 11.
