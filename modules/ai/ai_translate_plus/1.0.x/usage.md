AI Translate Plus extends AI Translate with per-bundle prompts and field exclusions.

---

AI Translate Plus **extends AI Translate** — allowing per-entity-type/bundle translation prompts per language
and disabling translation for selected fields, for finer control over AI translation. It depends on the AI and AI
Translate modules, and provides its own permissions.

Use it to fine-tune AI translation. It is an AI/multilingual feature. Security/data handling: AI translation
**sends content to the configured AI provider** (egress — including drafts; confirm acceptable) and relies on the
AI module for credentials (secrets). It has no access-control role beyond its permission. Configure the translation
prompts.
