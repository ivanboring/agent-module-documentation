AI Agent Modes adds a mode selector that steers an AI agent toward a curated subset of sub-agents.

---

AI Agent Modes **steers an AI agent by mode** — a mode selector that prepends a scoped instruction so the AI
orchestrator routes work to the right curated subset of sub-agents. It depends on the AI, AI Agents, AI Assistant
API, AI Chatbot and Canvas AI modules, and provides its own permissions, in the AI package.

Use it to guide AI-agent routing. It is an AI/orchestration feature. Security/data handling: it operates within the
AI agent stack, which **sends prompts/content to the configured AI provider** (egress) — confirm acceptable and
store provider credentials as secrets (via the AI module). It has no access-control role beyond its permission.
Configure the agent modes.
