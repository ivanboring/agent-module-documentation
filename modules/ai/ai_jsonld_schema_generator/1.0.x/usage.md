AI JSON-LD Schema Generator generates and attaches Schema.org JSON-LD to content using AI.

---

AI JSON-LD Schema Generator generates and attaches Schema.org JSON-LD to content using AI. It builds on Drupal's AI module and depends on `node`, `ai`, in the SEO package.

Use it for AI-assisted schema.org / structured data. It is an AI/integration feature. Security/data handling: it **sends
content to the configured AI provider** (external egress — confirm acceptable for the content), the provider **API key** is stored via the AI module's Key config (secret),
over HTTPS and AI output should be **reviewed** before use/publishing. It has no access-control role beyond its permission. Configure the AI
provider.
