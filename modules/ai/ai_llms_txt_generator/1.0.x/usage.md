AI LLMs.txt Generator generates a spec-compliant llms.txt from sitemap.xml using AI.

---

AI LLMs.txt Generator **generates llms.txt from the sitemap using AI** — producing a spec-compliant `llms.txt`
(a guide for large language models) derived from `sitemap.xml` via AI providers. It depends on the AI and Simple
Sitemap modules, and provides its own permissions.

Use it to publish an AI-generated llms.txt. It is an SEO/AI feature. Security/data handling: it **sends sitemap/
content data to the AI provider** (egress) to generate the file, and the resulting **llms.txt is published for LLM
crawlers** — include only public content; store AI credentials as secrets. It has no access-control role beyond
its permission. Configure the AI provider.
