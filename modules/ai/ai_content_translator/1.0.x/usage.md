AI Content Translator translates entities, terms and UI strings via AI.

---

AI Content Translator translates content entities, taxonomy terms and interface strings into other languages using AI — integrating with core content-translation and locale so a site's content and UI can be machine-translated through the AI module, with human review in the loop.

Translation sends content to the configured AI provider (cost + data leaves the site). Permissions cover administration (`administer ai content translator`) and translating (`translate content with ai`). Depends on core `language`, `content_translation`, and `locale`; requires Drupal 11.1+.
