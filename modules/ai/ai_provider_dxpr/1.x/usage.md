AI Provider: DXPR enables the use of DXPR AI as a provider for the Drupal AI module.

---

The Drupal AI module abstracts AI providers behind a common interface. AI Provider: DXPR registers DXPR AI as one such provider, so AI features can route through DXPR. It needs DXPR credentials (a key to keep out of plain config), and content sent to it leaves your infrastructure to DXPR (governance for confidential content). It is a provider plugin — infrastructure for the AI module.
