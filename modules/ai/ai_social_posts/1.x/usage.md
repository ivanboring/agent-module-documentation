AI Social Posts provides a content entity for managing social posts and publishes them to many platforms (X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, Reddit, TikTok, YouTube, Bluesky, Medium, Substack and more) via per-platform submodules.

---

Publishing content to many social networks from one place, with AI assistance, is a marketing efficiency. AI Social Posts provides a social-post content entity and publishes to a wide set of platforms through per-platform submodules (X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram Reels/Story, Reddit, TikTok, YouTube/Shorts, Bluesky, Medium, Substack, Hacker News, a newsletter channel, and more). Each platform integration needs that platform's API credentials/tokens — a set of sensitive secrets to keep out of plain config (a Key entity / environment); these tokens can post as your accounts, so protect them as you would any posting credential. Content generated/assisted by AI involves an AI provider key and data leaving to that provider. And publishing to social platforms is an outward-facing action — confirm what is auto-posted and to which accounts, since a misconfiguration posts to the wrong place. Enable only the platform submodules you use, and secure each platform's credentials.
