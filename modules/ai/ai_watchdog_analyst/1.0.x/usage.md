AI Watchdog Analyst uses AI to interpret dblog entries and suggest fixes.

---

AI Watchdog Analyst analyzes Drupal logs (the Watchdog/dblog log) using AI to suggest solutions — reading log entries (errors, warnings) and using AI to explain likely causes and recommend fixes, helping operators triage site issues faster.

Log entries are sent to the configured AI provider (cost + data egress — logs can contain sensitive data, so consider what is sent). Depends on core `dblog` and `ai`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
