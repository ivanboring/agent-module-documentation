Denormalizes entity data.

---

Denormalizer denormalizes entity data — transforming/flattening entity data into a denormalized form (for export, indexing, or downstream consumption), the inverse of Drupal's normalizers, useful in data pipelines and integrations. It's a developer/data-transformation tool. Supports Drupal 8.8 through 11.
