Convivial Profiler provides personalization/profiling capabilities.

---

Convivial Profiler **provides visitor personalization/profiling** — building a behavioral profile of a
visitor (interests, actions) to personalize content/experience, part of the Convivial toolkit. It depends on
Convivial Core, provides its own permissions, in the Convivial package.

Use it for on-site personalization. It is a personalization/marketing feature with **significant privacy
implications**: profiling visitors collects **behavioral/personal data** — depending on where the profile is
built (client and/or server) and stored, you must disclose it in your privacy policy, obtain **consent** where
required (profiling/tracking is regulated under GDPR/ePrivacy), and let users opt out; avoid profiling
sensitive categories. It has no access-control role beyond its permission. Configure the profiler and consent
handling.
