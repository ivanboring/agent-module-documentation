Conditional 404 Pages lets site builders configure different custom 404 pages depending on conditions such as the requested path.

---

A single 404 page does not fit every section — a docs area, a shop, a language section may each want their own not-found page. Conditional 404 Pages serves different 404 pages by condition (e.g. path prefix). It is a routing/UX feature. The standard 404 consideration applies: a 404 page must not leak sensitive information and should not reveal whether a path exists in a way that aids enumeration beyond what a normal 404 does. The conditional pages are admin-configured content. Confirm the conditions match intent and the pages contain no diagnostic detail.
