Cookie Consent Notice adds a text notification and prompt to update cookie settings for hidden elements.

---

Cookie Consent Notice adds a **cookie-consent notice** — a banner/prompt informing visitors about cookies
and letting them update cookie settings, and hiding/gating designated elements until consent is given. It
helps with GDPR/ePrivacy-style consent presentation, in the Custom package.

Use it to present a cookie notice and defer cookie-setting elements until consent. It is a privacy/consent UI
feature. Important expectation: it is a **consent presentation/UX** tool — the **effectiveness of consent
depends on you actually gating the cookie-setting scripts** behind it (a banner alone does not make a site
compliant; ensure trackers don't fire before consent). It stores the visitor's choice (typically a cookie).
It has no access-control role. Configure the notice and gated elements.
