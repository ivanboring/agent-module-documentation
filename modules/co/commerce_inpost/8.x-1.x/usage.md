Commerce InPost provides an InPost shipping provider for Drupal Commerce.

---

Commerce InPost provides an **InPost shipping provider for Drupal Commerce** — offering InPost delivery
(notably parcel-locker/Paczkomat) as a shipping method, with rate and locker selection. It depends on Commerce,
Commerce Checkout, Commerce Shipping and Commerce Order, in the Commerce (contrib) package.

Use it to offer InPost shipping. It is an e-commerce/shipping feature. Security/data handling: it calls the
**InPost API** — store the **API credentials** as secrets over HTTPS, and customer address/parcel data is sent
to InPost (external egress inherent to shipping). It has no access-control role. Configure the InPost shipping
method and credentials.
