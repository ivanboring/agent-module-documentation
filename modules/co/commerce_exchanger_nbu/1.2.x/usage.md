Commerce Exchanger NBU provides exchange rates from NBU.

---

Commerce Exchanger NBU provides **currency exchange rates from the National Bank of Ukraine (NBU)** for the
Commerce Exchanger module — so Drupal Commerce can convert prices using official NBU rates. It depends on the
Commerce Exchanger module, in the Commerce (contrib) package.

Use it to source NBU exchange rates. It is an e-commerce/integration feature; it fetches public rate data from
NBU (external, no credentials typically) and it has no access-control role. Configure NBU as the rate provider.
