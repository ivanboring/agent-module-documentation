Content Reminders provides a system for notifying editors and administrators about content.

---

Content Reminders gives editors and administrators a **reminder system for content** — configuring
notifications about content (for example, "review this page by a date" or content-lifecycle reminders), so
important content maintenance isn't forgotten. It provides its own permissions, in the Other package.

Use it to schedule content-review reminders. It is an editorial-workflow/administration feature; reminders/
notifications are governed by its permissions and it has no access-control role beyond that. Configure the
reminders.
