Commerce Quantity Increments lets products be sold in fixed quantity increments (e.g. multiples of 6), validating the cart quantity.

---

Some products only sell in packs or multiples — a case of 6, a minimum of 10. Commerce Quantity Increments enforces quantity increments and minimums on products, validating the cart. It is a Commerce configuration feature with no unusual security surface; the validation runs at add-to-cart and checkout. Confirm the increment/minimum rules match how the products are actually sold.
