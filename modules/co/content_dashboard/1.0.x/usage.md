Content Dashboard offers a dashboard to facilitate content management.

---

Content Dashboard **offers a dashboard for content management** — a landing/overview screen giving editors
quick access to content lists, counts and common actions, to streamline day-to-day editorial work. It provides
its own permissions.

Use it to give editors a content overview. It is an administration/editorial feature; its dashboard is gated by
its permission and shows content the user can access (lists follow access), and it has no access-control role
beyond that. Configure the dashboard.
