Commerce Cart Estimate provides a form for estimating taxes and shipping in the cart before checkout.

---

Shoppers want to know shipping and tax before committing to checkout. Commerce Cart Estimate adds a cart form to estimate them from a location. It is a Commerce UX feature with no unusual security surface — it computes estimates from the same tax/shipping configuration used at checkout. Confirm the estimate matches the actual checkout calculation to avoid surprising customers.
