Commerce Quick Node Clone quickly clones a Commerce product with all of its fields, for fast duplication of similar products.

---

Creating many similar products is faster by cloning than from scratch. Commerce Quick Node Clone duplicates a Commerce product with its fields. It is a Commerce editorial convenience. The consideration is that cloning copies all fields — confirm it does not clone anything that should be unique (a SKU, a stock count) without adjustment, and that clone access is restricted to those who should create products.
