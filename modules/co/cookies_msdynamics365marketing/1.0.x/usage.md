Anonymize callbacks for Dynamics 365 forms under COOKiES consent.

---

COOKiES MS Dynamics 365 Marketing Anonymize provides anonymize callbacks for Dynamics 365 Forms with the COOKiES Consent Management cookie banner — so a Dynamics 365 Marketing form is loaded in an anonymized/blocked state until the visitor grants consent through the COOKiES banner, keeping the site GDPR-compliant. Depends on `cookies`; supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
