Commerce Shipping Best Rate filters shipping rates presented to the customer based on service.

---

Commerce Shipping Best Rate **filters the shipping rates presented to the customer** — for example,
showing only the best (cheapest) rate per shipping service, or otherwise narrowing a long list of computed
rates to the most relevant ones. It depends on Commerce Shipping, in the Commerce (shipping) package.

Use it to simplify shipping-rate choices at checkout. It is an e-commerce/shipping feature; rates are computed
by Commerce Shipping (this module only filters what's displayed) and it has no access-control role. Configure
the rate-filtering behaviour.
