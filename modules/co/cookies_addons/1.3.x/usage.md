Cookies Addons provides additional features for the COOKiES module, including consent-gated embeds, blocks, fields, paragraphs and views.

---

Cookies Addons extends the COOKiES consent-management module with additional features — consent-gated
integrations for embedded iframes/videos, blocks, fields, paragraphs and views, so more content types can be
withheld until the visitor gives cookie/consent. It ships submodules (blocks, embed_iframe, embed_video,
fields, paragraphs, views) and depends on the COOKiES module, in the COOKiES package.

Use it to gate more content behind cookie consent. It is a privacy/consent feature: its purpose is to
**prevent third-party content (and its cookies/trackers) from loading until consent is given**, which is its
security/privacy value (GDPR/ePrivacy compliance). It has no access-control role. Configure which content is
consent-gated.
