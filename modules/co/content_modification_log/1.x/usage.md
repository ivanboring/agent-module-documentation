Content Modification Log implements an exportable, date-filterable log of content modifications — who changed what content and when.

---

Compliance and editorial oversight need a record of content changes. Content Modification Log keeps an exportable, date-filterable log of modifications. It is an audit tool. Two considerations: the log records editorial activity (who did what), so it is itself sensitive — restrict who can read and export it; and an export is a concentrated record, so handle exported logs as sensitive data. As an audit trail it supports accountability; keep it admin-gated and set a retention approach.
