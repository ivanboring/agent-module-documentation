Manage Telegram media items inside COOKiES consent management.

---

COOKiES Telegram Media is a submodule of COOKiES to manage Telegram media items (provided by the `telegram_media_type` module) inside COOKiES consent management — so embedded Telegram media is blocked until the visitor consents via the COOKiES banner, for GDPR compliance. Depends on `cookies` and `telegram_media_type`; supports Drupal 9.3+, 10, and 11.
