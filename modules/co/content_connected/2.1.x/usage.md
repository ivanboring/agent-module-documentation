Content Connected shows how content is connected with other content.

---

Content Connected **shows how a piece of content is connected to other content** — surfacing where an entity
is referenced/used (incoming and outgoing relationships), so editors understand impact before editing/deleting.
It depends on core Field, provides its own permissions.

Use it to see content relationships/usage. It is a content-editing/authoring aid; the relationship report
reflects references (following access) and it has no access-control role beyond its permission. View the
connections for an entity.
