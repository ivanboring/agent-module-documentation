Content Reviewed Date tracks when content was last reviewed and surfaces stale content.

---

Content Reviewed Date **tracks when content was last reviewed** — recording an editorial "last reviewed"
date per node and surfacing stale content that needs attention, for content-governance/freshness. It depends on
core Node and Datetime, provides its own permissions.

Use it to keep content fresh. It is a content-editing/governance feature; it stores a review date and reports on
staleness (editor-facing) and has no access-control role beyond its permission. Configure the reviewed-date
tracking.
