Coupon After Order generates a coupon after purchase.

---

Coupon After Order **generates a Commerce promotion coupon after a purchase** — automatically creating a
coupon (for a configured promotion) when an order completes, e.g. as a thank-you/next-purchase reward. It depends
on Commerce, Order, Price, Promotion and State Machine, provides its own permissions, in the Commerce package.

Use it to reward customers with post-purchase coupons. It is an e-commerce/marketing feature. Security note:
generated coupons have **monetary value** — ensure the coupon's promotion has appropriate limits (usage count,
expiry, single-use per customer) so auto-generated coupons can't be abused/shared for unlimited discounts. It
has no access-control role beyond its permission. Configure the coupon generation and its promotion limits.
