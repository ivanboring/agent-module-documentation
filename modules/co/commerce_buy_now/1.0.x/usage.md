Commerce Buy Now adds a 'buy now' button that adds a product to the cart and redirects straight to checkout, skipping the cart page.

---

For single-item or impulse purchases, sending the customer through the cart page adds friction. Commerce Buy Now adds a button that adds the product to the cart and goes straight to checkout in one step. It is a Commerce UX enhancement with no unusual security surface — it uses the standard add-to-cart and checkout flow, just chained. Confirm it behaves correctly with products that require options or quantity selection, since 'buy now' bypasses the cart where those are usually chosen.
