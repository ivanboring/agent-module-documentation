Commerce Chase integrates Chase card payment processing with Drupal Commerce.

---

Commerce Chase provides Commerce integration for Chase — a payment gateway that processes card payments through Chase (Orbital/Paymentech), letting a Drupal Commerce store charge cards via Chase's processor.

As a direct/onsite card gateway, card data flows through the merchant's PCI-compliant configuration; store the Chase API credentials securely (env-backed) and serve checkout over HTTPS. Depends on `commerce_payment`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
