Content Telemetry surfaces per-content performance insights and recommendations for editors.

---

Content Telemetry & Performance Insights captures performance telemetry correlated with content and presents it in editor-friendly insights and recommendations — helping non-developers understand which pages/content are slow and what to improve, without reading raw metrics. It bridges performance data and editorial workflow.

Permissions cover viewing (`view content telemetry`), administration (`administer content telemetry`), and clearing data (`clear content telemetry`). Depends on core `node`, `views`, and `system`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
