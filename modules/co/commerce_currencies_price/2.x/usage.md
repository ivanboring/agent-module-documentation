Commerce Currencies Price adds a Commerce price field variant that stores a price for every enabled currency, for manually-set multi-currency pricing.

---

Automatic currency conversion does not always give the prices a merchant wants — round numbers, market-specific pricing. Commerce Currencies Price provides a price field holding a value per enabled currency, so a product can have manually-set prices in each currency. It is a Commerce pricing feature with no unusual security surface. Confirm all enabled currencies have prices set, since a missing currency price means no price in that market.
