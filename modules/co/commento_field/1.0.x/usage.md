Commento Field embeds the Commento third-party comment widget via a field.

---

Commento Field integrates Commento — a lightweight, privacy-focused third-party commenting service — into Drupal as a field, so content can display a Commento comment thread instead of core comments. Visitors comment through the embedded Commento widget.

Permissions cover toggling (`toggle commento comments`) and viewing (`view commento comments`). The widget loads Commento's third-party script; review privacy/data-flow. Supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
