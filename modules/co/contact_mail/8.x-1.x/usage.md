Contact Mail provides a contact mail formatter, adjusting how contact-form emails are formatted.

---

Contact Mail provides a formatter for contact-form emails — adjusting how the emails generated by
Drupal's contact forms are formatted/structured. It is configured at `synmail.config` and is in the Mail
package. It is a small utility for shaping contact-form mail output.

Use it to customize contact-form email formatting. It operates on outbound contact-form mail; as with any
mail customization, review the configured formatting (contact emails contain the submitter's message and
possibly their email address, so handle that appropriately). It has no content-access role. Configure the
mail formatting.
