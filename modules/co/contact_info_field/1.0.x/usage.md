Contact Info Field provides a multi-value field to collect contact info.

---

Contact Info Field provides a **multi-value field type for contact information** — letting an entity store
one or more contact entries (e.g. phone, email, address label) in a single structured field. It is in the
Custom package.

Use it to attach contact details to content. It is a fields/content feature; values follow normal field
sanitization on display and it has no access-control role. Add the contact-info field to an entity.
