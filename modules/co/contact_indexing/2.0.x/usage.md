Adds a robots meta tag to contact forms to control search-engine indexing.

---

Contact Form Indexing adds a robots meta tag for indexing on the contact form — so a site can control whether contact form pages are indexed by search engines (e.g. noindex), which core doesn't expose out of the box. It's a small SEO/crawler-control add-on. Supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
