Basic Ads provides simple ad management with placements, scheduling and image/link ads.

---

Basic Ads **provides simple ad management** — creating banner/image/link ads with placements, scheduling and
targeting, so a site can run its own basic ad campaigns without a third-party ad server. It depends on core Node,
Taxonomy, Datetime, Image, Text, Link, Views and Block, provides its own permissions, in the Advertisement
package.

Use it to run simple on-site ads. It is a content/marketing feature; ad content is admin/editor-managed and it
has no access-control role beyond its permission. Note: ads are essentially trusted markup/links placed by admins
(keep ad creation to trusted users, since ads render on pages). Configure the ads and placements.
