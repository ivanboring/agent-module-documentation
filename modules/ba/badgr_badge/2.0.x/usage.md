Badgr Badge integrates Drupal with badgr.com to issue and manage open badges.

---

Badgr Badge connects Drupal to the Badgr (badgr.com) open-badge API, letting a site issue, store and manage digital badges (Open Badges) for users — useful for e-learning, certifications and community recognition. Badges are attached to content/files within Drupal.

Permissions separate administration (`administer badgr badge`) from issuance (`create badgr badge`). The Badgr API credentials should be stored securely (env-backed); restrict badge creation to trusted roles. Depends on core `node` and `file`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
