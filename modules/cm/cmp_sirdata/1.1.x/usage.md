Sirdata CMP integrates the Sirdata Consent Management Platform.

---

Sirdata CMP **integrates the Sirdata Consent Management Platform** — adding Sirdata's cookie-consent banner/
CMP so the site can collect and manage visitor consent for cookies/tracking (IAB TCF-style). It provides its own
permissions, in the Custom package.

Use it for cookie-consent compliance via Sirdata. It is a privacy/compliance feature. Data-handling: it loads
**Sirdata's third-party CMP script** (which manages consent and may itself set cookies / contact Sirdata), and
consent decisions govern whether other tracking runs — so wire your analytics/marketing tags to respect the
CMP's consent signal. It has no content or access role beyond its permission. Configure the Sirdata CMP.
