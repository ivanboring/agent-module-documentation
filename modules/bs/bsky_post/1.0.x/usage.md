Bluesky Post publishes Drupal content to a Bluesky account.

---

Bluesky Post (bsky_post) posts Drupal content to Bluesky — so when content is published, a post is created on a configured Bluesky account (via the bsky module), automating social sharing to the Bluesky/AT Protocol network.

Bluesky credentials are handled by the bsky module (store securely, env-backed). Permissions cover configuration (`administer bsky_post configuration`) and posting (`post to bluesky`). Depends on `bsky`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
