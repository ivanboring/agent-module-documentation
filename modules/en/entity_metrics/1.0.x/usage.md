Record page views and downloads for entities.

---

Entity Metrics records page views and downloads for entities on your Drupal site — counting how often each entity is viewed and its files downloaded, so editors can see popularity/engagement metrics per entity.

Any external analytics credentials are handled via a Key entity (`key` dependency, env-backed). Depends on `key`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
