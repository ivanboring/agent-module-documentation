Entity Notes provides the ability to add notes to content entities, for editorial annotations and internal comments.

---

Entity Notes lets editors attach notes to content entities — internal annotations, editorial
comments or reminders associated with a node/entity that aren't part of the public content. It depends
on Views (for listing notes) and provides its own permissions. Notes give editorial teams a place to
record context ("needs legal review", "update after launch") against the content they refer to.

Use it for editorial workflow annotations and internal collaboration on content. The security-relevant
point is that notes are internal information: gate viewing/adding notes with the module's permissions so
only appropriate roles can see them (notes may contain sensitive internal remarks that must not leak to
the public or unauthorized editors). Confirm notes aren't exposed through public displays.
