Entity Share Trash Ignore integrates with the Trash module to ignore trashed entities on entity share.

---

Entity Share Trash Ignore integrates **Entity Share** with the **Trash** module — so entities that are in
the trash are **ignored during Entity Share sync** (not pulled/pushed), preventing trashed content from being
re-shared between sites. It depends on Entity Share Client and Trash, in the Web services package.

Use it on Entity Share sites that also use Trash. It is a content-sync/integration feature; sharing access
follows Entity Share's configuration and it has no access-control role of its own. Enable it to skip trashed
entities during sync.
