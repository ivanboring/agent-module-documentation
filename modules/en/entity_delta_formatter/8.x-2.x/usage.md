Entity Delta Formatter displays a specified delta of a multi-value field.

---

Entity Delta Formatter provides a **field formatter that displays a specified delta** — showing only a
chosen item (by index/delta) of a multi-value field, e.g. render just the first or Nth value. It is in the
Fields package.

Use it to show a single item of a multi-value field. It is a content-display feature affecting field rendering;
values follow normal field sanitization and it has no content or access role. Configure the delta on the
formatter.
