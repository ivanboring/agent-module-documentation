Visualizes dependencies between entities.

---

Entity Dependency Visualizer renders a visual graph of dependencies between entities — showing how content/config entities reference and depend on each other, helping developers and site builders understand and untangle complex relationships before edits or deletions. It's a developer/inspection tool. Supports Drupal 8 through 11.
