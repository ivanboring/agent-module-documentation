Entity Lifecycle manages entity lifecycle including outdated-content detection and review status.

---

Entity Lifecycle **manages entity lifecycle** — detecting outdated content and managing review status so
editors can track and act on content that needs review/refresh, with Views integration. It depends on core Node
and Views, and provides its own permissions.

Use it to govern content freshness/review. It is a content-editing/governance feature; it tracks review state and
surfaces outdated content (editor-facing) and has no access-control role beyond its permission. Configure the
lifecycle/review settings.
