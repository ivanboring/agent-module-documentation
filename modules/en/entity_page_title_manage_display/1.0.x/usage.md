A dedicated view mode to manage the entity page title display.

---

Entity Page Title Manage Display adds a dedicated view mode to manage the title display for complex entity page titles on full-page display — so instead of the plain title, site builders can control how the title field is rendered (formatter, wrapping, extra fields) on the entity's full view. Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
