Applies capitalization to entity field values.

---

Entity Field Capitalization applies capitalization of any field values of an entity — transforming a field's text to a chosen capitalization (uppercase, title case, etc.) on save/display, so content follows a consistent style without manual editing. It's a content-transformation helper. Supports Drupal 8 through 11.
