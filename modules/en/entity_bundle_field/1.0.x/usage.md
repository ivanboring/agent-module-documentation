Entity Bundle Field provides **additional functionality for entity bundle fields** — helper support around
managing fields at the bundle level (e.g. bundle-scoped field handling in the storage/UI), in the Administration
package.

Use it as a site-building/developer aid for bundle fields. It is a fields/admin feature; it has no content or
access role. Use it when managing bundle fields.
