An entity information tab filled by custom plugins.

---

Entity Information provides an information tab for entities that can be filled with content from custom plugins — so each entity gets an extra 'Information' tab in its local tasks where pluggable panels display relevant data (metadata, related items, computed info) chosen by the site. Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
