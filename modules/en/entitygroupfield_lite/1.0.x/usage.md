Entity Group Field lite links content entities to Group entities via a field.

---

Entity Group Field lite **links content entities to groups** — a lightweight way to associate content
entities with Group (from the Group module) via a field, without the full Group-content plugin machinery. It
depends on the Group and Form Options Attributes modules.

Use it to relate content to groups simply. It is a site-building/access-adjacent feature: because Group governs
membership-based access, how content is linked to groups can affect what group members see — so verify the linkage
composes with your Group access setup as intended. It has no access-control role of its own. Configure the group
field.
