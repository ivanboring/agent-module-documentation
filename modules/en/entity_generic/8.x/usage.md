Generic entity functionalities and helpers.

---

Entity Generic provides generic entity functionalities and helpers — reusable base classes, traits and utilities for building custom content entities, reducing boilerplate for module developers who define their own entity types. It's a developer/framework module. Depends on `entity`; supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
