Entity Notify emails or Telegram-messages the site administrator when entities of configured types are created or changed.

---

Operators sometimes want to know the moment something happens — a new user registers, a form entity is submitted, a node of a certain type is created. Entity Notify sends the administrator a notification (email or Telegram) on entity create/change for configured types. It needs notification-channel configuration; for Telegram, a bot token is a credential to keep out of plain config. The notifications can contain entity data, so where they are sent (an inbox, a Telegram chat) inherits the sensitivity of that data — direct them to an appropriately private channel.
