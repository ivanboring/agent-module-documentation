Adds the vocabulary name to taxonomy term form titles.

---

Enhanced taxonomy form titles adds a vocabulary mention in the title of taxonomy term forms — so when adding/editing a term the page title shows which vocabulary it belongs to, reducing confusion on sites with many vocabularies. It's a small editorial-UX improvement. Supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
