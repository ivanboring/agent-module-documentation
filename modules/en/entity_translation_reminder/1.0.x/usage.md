Entity Translation Reminder reminds content editors that translations need updating.

---

Entity Translation Reminder **reminds editors to update translations** — flagging when a source entity has
changed so its translations may be out of date, prompting editors to refresh them. It depends on core Content
Translation and provides its own permissions.

Use it to keep translations in sync. It is a content-editing/multilingual feature; it surfaces reminders
(editor-facing) and has no access-control role beyond its permission. Configure the translation reminders.
