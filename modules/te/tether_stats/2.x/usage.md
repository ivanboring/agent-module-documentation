Tether Stats is a self-contained site-statistics system tracking hits, impressions and other metrics without a third-party analytics service.

---

Some sites want usage statistics but do not want to send visitor data to Google Analytics or another third party — for privacy, policy, or self-hosting reasons. Tether Stats tracks hits, impressions and similar metrics itself, storing them in Drupal. That keeps analytics data in-house, which is a privacy advantage, but it also means the site is now recording visitor activity, so the usual considerations apply: the tracking data can be personal in aggregate, it should have a retention approach, and its reports should be restricted to staff. Self-hosting analytics shifts the privacy responsibility onto the site rather than removing it.
