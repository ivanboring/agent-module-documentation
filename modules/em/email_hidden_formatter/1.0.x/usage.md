A field formatter that hides an email until a click reveals it.

---

Email Hidden Formatter provides a formatter which hides the email element and, instead of rendering a link, shows a trigger that reveals the email on click — reducing exposure of email addresses to scrapers/bots while keeping them accessible to real users. Depends on core `field`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
