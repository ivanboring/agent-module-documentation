Email Obfuscator obfuscates emails any and everywhere.

---

Email Obfuscator obfuscates email addresses wherever they appear in the site's output — encoding/
scrambling `mailto:` addresses so simple email-harvesting bots don't scrape them directly from the page
markup, reducing spam. It is in the Custom package.

Use it to lightly deter email harvesting. **Understand the limits: this is a scraping deterrent, not real
protection.** The email still has to be delivered to the browser to be usable/clickable, so a determined
scraper (or anyone viewing the page/deobfuscating the markup) can still obtain it — it reduces casual
automated harvesting but does not keep an address private. Don't rely on it to protect an address that
shouldn't be exposed. It has no access-control role. Enable it to obfuscate output emails.
