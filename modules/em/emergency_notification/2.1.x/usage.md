Emergency Notification adds emergency notification functionality, such as a site-wide alert banner.

---

Emergency Notification adds emergency-notification functionality — letting administrators publish a
prominent site-wide alert (e.g. an emergency banner for closures, incidents, urgent notices) that displays
across the site until dismissed/cleared. It is configured at `emergency_notification.admin_settings` and
provides its own permissions.

Use it to broadcast urgent site-wide alerts. It is a content/notification feature; the alert is admin-
authored content shown to visitors, and it has no access-control role. Gate who can publish emergency
notifications with its permissions (publishing a site-wide alert is a high-visibility action — restrict to
trusted editors). Configure the notification.
