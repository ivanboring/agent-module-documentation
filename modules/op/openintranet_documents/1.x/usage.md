OpenIntranet Documents provides hierarchical document management with folders, part of the OpenIntranet distribution.

---

An intranet needs a document store — folders, files, organised hierarchically, shared within the organisation. OpenIntranet Documents provides that, part of the OpenIntranet distribution. Like any document-management system, its security is its access model: documents and folders hold potentially-confidential organisational content, so who can view, add and manage them must be controlled, and (as with FolderShare) files should be in private storage served through access checks rather than directly fetchable. Confirm the access model matches your organisation's confidentiality needs, and that document files are not directly reachable by URL bypassing the folder access.
