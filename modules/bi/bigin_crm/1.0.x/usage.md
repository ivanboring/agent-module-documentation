Bigin CRM integrates Drupal with Zoho Bigin CRM (sync contacts/leads to Bigin).

---

Bigin CRM **integrates Drupal with Zoho Bigin CRM** — syncing contacts/leads (e.g. from forms) into Zoho
Bigin, the small-business CRM. It provides its own permissions, in the Bigin package.

Use it to feed leads into Bigin. It is an integration/CRM feature. Security/data handling: it **sends contact/lead
data (PII) to the Bigin API** (external egress — disclose per privacy policy) and authenticates with **Bigin/Zoho
OAuth credentials** (store as secrets — env/Key — over HTTPS). It has no access-control role beyond its
permission. Configure the Bigin credentials.
