Birthday Wish Mail sends birthday wish mail to users automatically.

---

Birthday Wish Mail **automatically emails a birthday greeting to users on their birthday** — using a
configured birthday field and cron to send a templated wish when the date matches. It depends on the Token
module.

Use it to send automated birthday emails. It is a user-engagement feature. Privacy/operational notes: it uses
users' **birthday (personal data)** and email addresses, so handle that data per your privacy policy; and it
relies on **cron running reliably** to send on the right day. It has no access-control role. Configure the
birthday field and email template.
