Block Visibility Days lets blocks show/hide based on days and dates.

---

Block Visibility Days enhances block visibility settings by adding days conditions — so a block can be shown or hidden based on the day of week or a date range (e.g. a promo block only on weekends, or during a campaign window), extending core's block visibility with time/day rules.

Note this controls block DISPLAY (when a block is shown), not data access. It's a block/site-building enhancement with no content or access role of its own. Supports Drupal 8 through 11.
