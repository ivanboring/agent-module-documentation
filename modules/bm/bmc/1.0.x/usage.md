Buy Me a Coffee provides a Buy Me a Coffee module.

---

Buy Me a Coffee (bmc) adds a **"Buy Me a Coffee" donation button/widget** as a block — embedding the BMC
widget so visitors can support the site owner via the Buy Me a Coffee service. It depends on core Block,
provides its own permissions.

Use it to add a BMC donation button. It is a third-party integration/user-engagement feature — it loads **Buy
Me a Coffee's third-party JavaScript/widget** (trust the vendor; the donation flow and payment happen on BMC's
side), and it has no access-control role beyond its permission. Configure the BMC username/widget.
