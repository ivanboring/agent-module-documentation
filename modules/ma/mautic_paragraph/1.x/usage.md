Mautic Paragraph creates a Mautic paragraph type and block type for embedding Mautic marketing-automation forms/content in Drupal.

---

Mautic is an open-source marketing-automation platform; embedding its forms or dynamic content in Drupal pages connects the two. Mautic Paragraph provides a Mautic paragraph and block type for that. It loads Mautic content/scripts, which is the consideration: embedding Mautic means a third-party marketing script on the page (tracking implications — gate behind consent where required), and any Mautic connection details kept secure. For a site running Mautic it embeds its content natively.
