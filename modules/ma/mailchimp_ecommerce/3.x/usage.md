Mailchimp E-Commerce integrates Drupal Commerce carts and orders with Mailchimp's e-commerce features for marketing automation.

---

Mailchimp's e-commerce features drive marketing automation from purchase behaviour — abandoned-cart emails, product recommendations. Mailchimp E-Commerce syncs Commerce carts and orders to Mailchimp. It needs a Mailchimp API key (a credential to keep out of plain config) and forwards customer and order data — personal and commercial data — to Mailchimp, so the privacy posture (disclosure, consent for marketing) extends there. Confirm consent is captured before customers become marketing contacts and that the data sent matches what you intend to share.
