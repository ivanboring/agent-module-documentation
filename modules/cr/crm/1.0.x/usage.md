CRM (Contact Relationship Management) provides contact entities and related data structures for managing relationships within Drupal.

---

CRM (Contact Relationship Management) provides a native contact-management system in Drupal — contact
entities (with configurable contact types via the `crm_contact_type` collection) storing names, addresses,
phone, email and related data, building on Address, Name, Telephone, Inline Entity Form and Primary Entity
Reference. It gives organizations a place to manage people/relationships alongside their site. It provides
its own permissions and Drush commands.

Use it to manage contacts (members, donors, customers, leads) natively in Drupal. The security-relevant
point is that CRM data is **personal data (PII)** — names, contact details, relationships — so access to
contact entities must be carefully permission-gated (only appropriate staff should view/edit contacts),
and privacy/retention/consent obligations (e.g. GDPR) apply to storing this data. Verify the contact
access model matches your data-protection requirements. Configure contact types and fields.
