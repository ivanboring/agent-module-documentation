Critique and Review Content lets users review/critique other users' content for collaboration.

---

Critique and Review Content **lets users review or critique other users' content** — adding a
review/critique workflow so collaborators can give feedback on content, enriching editorial workflows. It works on
core 9–11.

Use it for collaborative content feedback. It is a user-engagement/workflow feature. Note: critiques are
**user-submitted content** tied to authors — apply normal input sanitization (it's user text) and consider who may
see/leave critiques, and moderate to prevent abuse. It has no explicit access-control role. Configure critique and
review.
