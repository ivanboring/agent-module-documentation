Summon an AI bot by mentioning it in content or comments.

---

Crux - The AI Mention Bot lets you summon an AI bot in Drupal by mentioning it in content or comments — when a user @-mentions the bot (via CKEditor mentions), Crux sends the context to the AI module and posts the bot's reply, so an AI assistant can participate in on-site discussions.

The AI provider is configured through the `ai` module (keys env-backed via a Key entity); because each mention triggers an LLM call, keep mention/comment permissions and rate limits tight to control cost. Depends on `ckeditor_mentions`, `ai`, and core `user`/`system`; supports Drupal 10.2+ and 11.
