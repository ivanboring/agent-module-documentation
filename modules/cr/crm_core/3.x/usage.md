CRM Core provides CRM entities (contacts, activities) with matching and user-sync submodules.

---

CRM Core is a native Drupal CRM framework: it models CRM data — contacts, activities, and relationships — as Drupal entities, and ships submodules for contact management (`crm_core_contact`), activity tracking (`crm_core_activity`), duplicate matching (`crm_core_match`), and syncing Drupal users to contacts (`crm_core_user_sync`), plus demo content. It lets a site manage customer/constituent relationships without an external CRM.

It's the base framework other integrations (e.g. crm_core_commerce) build on. Grant CRM administration only to trusted staff. No hard dependencies; supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
