Crouton provides a simple menu-based breadcrumb builder, deriving breadcrumbs from a chosen menu structure.

---

Breadcrumbs that reflect a site's menu hierarchy are more meaningful than the URL-based default. Crouton builds breadcrumbs from a chosen menu, so a page's breadcrumb trail follows its place in the menu. It is a navigation/display convenience with no security surface. Confirm the breadcrumb source menu matches the site's information architecture.
