Crossword adds a Crossword field that turns uploaded files into playable puzzles.

---

Crossword adds a Crossword field type — turning uploaded crossword files into interactive, playable
puzzles on the site, with extensive submodules (colors, contest, download, image, media, pseudofields,
status, token) for theming, contests, downloads and more. It depends on core File, in the Crossword package.

Use it to publish playable crosswords. It is a content-editing/interactive-content feature; the puzzle is
authored content (from uploaded files) rendered for visitors, and it has no access-control role. Add the
Crossword field and configure the submodules you need.
