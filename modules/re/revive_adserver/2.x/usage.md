Revive Adserver provides plugins for displaying ads served by a Revive Adserver instance.

---

Revive Adserver is a self-hosted, open-source ad server. Revive Adserver (this module) displays its ads in Drupal via plugins. It loads ad tags/scripts from the Revive instance, which is the consideration: ad tags can track users and render third-party content, so ad display should respect cookie/tracking consent where required, and the Revive instance's content is trusted to the extent you control it (self-hosted Revive is more controllable than a third-party network). Configure the ad zones and gate scripts behind consent as appropriate.
