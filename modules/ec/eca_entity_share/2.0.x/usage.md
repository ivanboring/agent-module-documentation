ECA Entity Share integrates ECA with the Entity Share module and its plugins.

---

ECA Entity Share integrates **ECA** (Events-Conditions-Actions) with **Entity Share** — exposing Entity
Share events/actions to ECA models (with `client` and `server` submodules), so content syndication between
sites can be driven by ECA automation. It is in the ECA package.

Use it to automate Entity Share syndication with ECA. It is an automation/content-sync feature; sharing access
and the sync itself are governed by **Entity Share** (this adds ECA orchestration), and it has no
access-control role of its own. Configure the ECA model and Entity Share.
