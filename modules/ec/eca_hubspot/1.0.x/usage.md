ECA actions for HubSpot CRM integration.

---

ECA HubSpot provides ECA actions for HubSpot CRM integration — so ECA (Event-Condition-Action) models can create/update HubSpot contacts and interact with the HubSpot CRM as part of automated, no-code workflows. HubSpot credentials are handled via `hubspot_api` (store them securely). Depends on `eca` and `hubspot_api`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
