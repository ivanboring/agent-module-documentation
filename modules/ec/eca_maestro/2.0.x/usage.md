Integrate ECA with the Maestro workflow engine.

---

ECA Maestro integrates ECA with the Maestro workflow module — bridging ECA (Event-Condition-Action) and Maestro's business-process workflow engine, so ECA models can drive or react to Maestro workflow tasks/transitions, combining no-code automation with structured business processes. Depends on `eca` and `eca_endpoint`; supports Drupal 9.5+, 10, and 11.
