Float Labels applies JS-enhanced floating labels to form fields — the label sits in the field and animates above it on focus/input.

---

Floating labels are a popular form pattern: the label starts as placeholder text inside the field and floats up when the user focuses or types, saving vertical space while keeping the label visible. Float Labels applies that to Drupal forms with configuration rather than custom CSS/JS. It is a front-end enhancement with no security surface. Confirm it works with your theme and that the floating behaviour does not harm accessibility — a floating label must remain a real, associated `<label>` for screen readers, not just placeholder text.
