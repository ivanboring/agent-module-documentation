Artisan Styleguide surfaces theme components and styles in a browsable style-guide page.

---

Artisan Styleguide gives front-end developers a living style guide: a page that showcases a theme's components, typography, colours and UI patterns in one place, so designers and developers can review and reuse the design system as they build. It serialises component data for display.

It's a theme-development aid with no content or access role of its own. Depends on core `serialization`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
