Arch dashboard provides the store management dashboard for the Arch commerce suite.

---

Arch dashboard is the store dashboard for Arch — a Drupal-native e-commerce platform — providing management views for products and orders (via arch_product and arch_order), so store operators can manage their Arch storefront from a dashboard.

Administration is gated by `administer store`. It's part of the Arch commerce suite. Depends on the `entity` module and Arch's `arch_product`/`arch_order`; supports Drupal 9.4, 10, and 11.
