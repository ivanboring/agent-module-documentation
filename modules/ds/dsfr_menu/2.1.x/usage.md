Creation and management of DSFR-styled menus.

---

DSFR Menus provides creation and management of menus that follow the DSFR (French State Design System), also working with DSFR child themes — so a site can build navigation menus that render with the correct DSFR markup/styling. Depends on core `block`, `menu_link_content`, `text`, and `dsfr_core`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
