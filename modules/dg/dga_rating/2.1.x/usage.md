DGA Rating provides an accessible 1-5 star rating widget with optional feedback and live stats.

---

DGA Rating **provides an accessible 1–5 star rating widget** — letting visitors rate content (1–5 stars) with
optional feedback and live statistics, built to the DGA design-system accessibility standard. It provides its own
permissions.

Use it to collect star ratings. It is a user-engagement feature. Data-handling notes: ratings/feedback are
**user-submitted** — sanitize any free-text feedback, apply anti-abuse/flood control to the rating endpoint so
totals can't be gamed, and expose only aggregate stats appropriately. It has its own permissions. Configure the
rating widget.
