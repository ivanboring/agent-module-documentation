Share buttons for the major networks. Simple Social Share provides them for content. As with any share widget, the privacy note is whether the buttons load third-party network scripts (which track visitors) or are simple share links — prefer plain share URLs over network SDKs where tracking is a concern. Confirm which approach it uses for your privacy posture.
