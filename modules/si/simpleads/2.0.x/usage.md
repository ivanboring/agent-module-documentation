SimpleAds is an advertisement module: it models ads, ad groups and campaigns as entities, serves them in blocks, and tracks impressions and clicks.

---

- Serve ads on the site.
- Model ads as entities.
- Group ads into campaigns.
- Track ad impressions.
- Track ad clicks.
- Place an ad block.
- Rotate ads in a group.
- Schedule an ad campaign.
- Report ad performance.
- Restrict ad administration.
- Manage house ads.
- Serve banner ads.
- Target ads by placement.
- Count clicks per ad.
- Publish and unpublish ads.
- Organise ads by group.
- Run a promotional campaign.
- Display sponsored content.
- Measure ad engagement.
- Control ad publishing by permission.
- Manage ad inventory.
- Serve responsive ads.
