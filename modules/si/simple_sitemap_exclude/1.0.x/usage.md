Simple XML sitemap exclude pattern adds path-pattern exclusions to Simple XML Sitemap, so URLs matching a pattern are kept out of the generated sitemap.

---

Simple XML Sitemap decides inclusion per entity bundle and per entity, which covers the ordinary cases and not the awkward ones. A sitemap should not list every facet combination a search page can generate, every paginated variant of an archive, a staging section, a set of thank-you pages that only make sense after a form submission, or paths generated by a module rather than by an entity. Excluding those one at a time is impossible when they are generated; a pattern handles the whole family. Version **1.0.0** on core `^10.2 || ^11.0`, requiring `simple_sitemap`. Why it matters is a search-engine argument rather than a tidiness one. A sitemap is a statement to a crawler about which URLs are worth its attention, and **crawl budget is finite** — a site that submits fifty thousand faceted permutations dilutes the signal for the two thousand pages it actually cares about, and duplicate or near-duplicate URLs in a sitemap are an active negative. The point of care is the mirror image: **an exclusion pattern is broader than it looks**, and a pattern that quietly removes a whole content section is a page that stops being found, discovered months later in a traffic report. Write patterns narrowly, and check the generated sitemap after changing one rather than trusting the pattern to mean what it looked like it meant.
