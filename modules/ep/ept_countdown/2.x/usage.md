EPT Countdown adds a countdown-timer paragraph type with an animated countdown, part of the Extra Paragraph Types family.

---

A countdown to an event, a sale, or a launch is a common landing-page element. EPT Countdown adds an animated countdown paragraph, part of the Extra Paragraph Types (EPT) family sharing ept_core. It is a display paragraph with no security surface. Confirm the target date/timezone behaviour matches your intent, since a countdown's correctness is entirely in its date handling.
