e-Plikt provides RSS export for entities affected by mandatory (legal) delivery.

---

e-Plikt **provides RSS export for entities under mandatory delivery** — exposing content subject to legal-
deposit/mandatory-delivery obligations (e.g. to a national library) as an RSS feed with downloadable media. It
depends on core Media and the Media Entity Download module.

Use it to publish a legal-deposit RSS feed. It is a web-services/import-export feature. Data-handling note: the
feed **exposes entity content and downloadable media** to whoever can read it — ensure the feed's audience is
appropriate (restrict it if the content isn't meant to be public), and confirm only the intended
mandatory-delivery items are included. It has no access-control role. Configure the e-Plikt export.
