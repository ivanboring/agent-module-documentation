EPT Webform Popup provides an Extra Paragraph Types paragraph that shows a Webform in a popup when a button is clicked.

---

EPT Webform Popup is part of the Extra Paragraph Types (EPT) family — configurable, styled
paragraph types. This one renders a button that, when clicked, opens a chosen Webform in a popup/modal,
so a form (contact, signup, feedback) can be offered inline on a page without taking over the layout.
It depends on the EPT basic button, Paragraphs and Webform modules.

Use it to embed a call-to-action button that reveals a Webform in a modal on landing pages or content.
It is a content-display/site-building paragraph; the Webform it opens keeps its own access, handlers
and validation, and this module handles the button-and-popup presentation. No access-control role of
its own.
