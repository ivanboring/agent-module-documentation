Tab Panel Widget provides a responsive, accessible tab-panel library, with Quicktabs and Views integration submodules.

---

Tabbed interfaces are common but easy to build inaccessibly. Tab Panel Widget provides a responsive, accessibility-minded tab-panel implementation, with `tabpanelwidget_quicktabs` and `tabpanelwidget_views` submodules integrating it with Quicktabs and Views. It is a front-end UI library integration; its stated focus on accessibility is the selling point — confirm the ARIA roles and keyboard behaviour meet your requirements, since accessible tabs are the whole reason to prefer a library over hand-rolled markup.
