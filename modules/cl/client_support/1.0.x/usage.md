Client Support provides a support-contact feature for users.

---

Client Support allows users to contact support — providing a support/contact form or ticketing feature so site users can reach the support team, useful for client portals or membership sites that need a built-in support channel.

Permissions cover access (`access client support`) and administration (`administer client support`). Because support requests may contain personal data, restrict administration to support staff. Supports Drupal 10 and 11.
