IEF Complex Open is an Inline Entity Form complex widget variant that opens the 'add existing' entity form open by default.

---

Inline Entity Form's complex widget lets editors add referenced entities inline; IEF Complex Open is a variant that shows the 'add existing' form open by default, a small editorial-UX tweak for workflows where referencing existing entities is the common action. It is a widget with no security surface; it changes the form's initial state, not what can be referenced or who can edit.
