Happy New Year adds seasonal garland and falling-snow decoration to a site, as a festive display effect.

---

A purely festive module: it overlays garland and falling snow for a seasonal look. It is a front-end decoration with no security surface and no functional purpose beyond the effect. The only practical notes are performance (an animated overlay costs some client resources) and taste (it is seasonal, so it is usually enabled and disabled around the holidays). Nothing sensitive is involved.
