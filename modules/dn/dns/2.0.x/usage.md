Manage DNS zones and records as content entities.

---

DNS lets you manage DNS zones and records as content entities, with a pluggable record-type architecture (provider-sync architecture planned) — so a site can model DNS zones and their records (A, CNAME, MX, etc.) as Drupal entities, edit them in the admin UI, and (in future) sync to DNS providers. Depends on core `options`, `views`, and `field_ipaddress`; supports Drupal 10.3+, 11, and 12.
