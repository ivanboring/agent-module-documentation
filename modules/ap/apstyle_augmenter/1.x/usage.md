AP Style Augmenter provides a plugin to adjust date output to meet Associated Press (AP) style conventions.

---

AP style has specific rules for dates (abbreviated months, no leading zeros). AP Style Augmenter adjusts date output to those conventions, likely as an Augmentor plugin. It is a formatting/content utility with no security surface. Confirm the AP-style output matches the current AP stylebook rules for your date formats.
