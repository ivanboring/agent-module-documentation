Tarte au citron Eulerian provides an Eulerian plugin for the Tarte au citron module.

---

Tarte au citron Eulerian integrates Eulerian analytics with the Tarte au citron consent manager — so the
Eulerian tracking is loaded only after the visitor gives consent, gating that third-party analytics behind
cookie/consent. It depends on the Tarte au citron module, in the GDPR package.

Use it to make Eulerian analytics consent-compliant. It is a **privacy-positive** feature: it prevents the
Eulerian tracker (and its cookies) from loading until consent, supporting GDPR/ePrivacy compliance. It has no
access-control role. Configure the Eulerian consent service.
