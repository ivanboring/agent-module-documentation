Social Share provides contextually configurable social-share buttons for content.

---

Share buttons for the major networks are a standard content feature. Social Share provides configurable share buttons that adapt to context. It is a front-end feature; the privacy note common to share widgets is whether they load third-party network scripts (which can track visitors) or are simple share links — prefer plain share URLs over network SDKs where tracking is a concern. Confirm which approach this uses for your privacy posture.
