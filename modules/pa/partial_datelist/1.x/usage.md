Partial Datelist lets administrators hide specific date or time dropdowns (e.g. seconds, or day) in datelist widgets, for simpler date entry.

---

A full datelist widget shows year/month/day/hour/minute/second dropdowns, more than many fields need. Partial Datelist lets an administrator hide specific dropdowns, simplifying date entry to just what is relevant. It is a form-widget configuration with no security surface. Confirm the hidden components have sensible defaults, since a hidden dropdown still contributes a value (e.g. hiding seconds defaults them to zero).
