CK5 Webform enables embedding of Webforms in CKEditor 5.

---

CKEditor 5 Webform **lets editors embed Webforms into rich-text content** — adding a CKEditor 5 button/plugin
to insert a chosen webform inline in a body/text field, rendered on display. It depends on core CKEditor 5 and the
Webform module, in the CKEditor 5 package.

Use it to place webforms inside rich text. It is a content-editing feature; the embedded webform renders through
the webform system (which enforces its own access/handlers), and it has no access-control role. Add the webform
plugin to a CKEditor 5 toolbar.
