CKEditor5 Sup Fix prevents CKEditor 5 from mangling superscript and anchor nesting.

---

CKEditor5 Sup Fix **prevents CKEditor 5 from mangling `<sup>` and anchor nesting** — a small fix so
superscript markup and nested anchors survive CKEditor 5's editing/normalization instead of being altered or
stripped. It depends on core CKEditor 5.

Use it if CKEditor 5 breaks your superscript/anchor markup. It is a content-editing/WYSIWYG fix; it affects how
the editor preserves markup and has no content or access role. Enable it to keep sup/anchor markup intact.
