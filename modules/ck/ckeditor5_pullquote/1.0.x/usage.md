CKEditor5 Pullquote adds a button to insert <pullquote> elements, rendered via a display filter.

---

CKEditor5 Pullquote adds a toolbar button to CKEditor 5 that inserts a `<pullquote>` element; a display filter then transforms it into the appropriate styled span for rendering. It gives editors an easy way to add magazine-style pull quotes to rich text.

It's a CKEditor 5 plugin plus a text-format filter, with no content or access role of its own; ensure the filter is enabled on the relevant text formats. Depends on core `ckeditor5`; supports Drupal 10.5+ and 11.
