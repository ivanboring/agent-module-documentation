CKEditor Text Selection adds a CKEditor 5 plugin for working with text selections.

---

CKEditor Text Selection is a CKEditor 5 plugin that adds text-selection functionality to the editor — helping editors act on selected text within the WYSIWYG. It's a small editing enhancement.

It's a CKEditor 5 plugin with no content or access role of its own. Depends on core `ckeditor5`; supports Drupal 10, 11, and 12.
