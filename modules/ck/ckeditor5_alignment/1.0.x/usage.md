CKEditor5 Alignment Buttons surfaces alignment options as separate toolbar buttons.

---

CKEditor5 Alignment Buttons changes how text alignment is offered in CKEditor 5: instead of a single dropdown, each alignment (left/center/right/justify) is a separate toolbar button, giving editors one-click access. It's a small editor-UX enhancement.

It's a CKEditor 5 plugin with no content or access role of its own. Depends on core `ckeditor5`; supports Drupal 10 and 11.
