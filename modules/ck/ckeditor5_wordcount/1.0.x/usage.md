CKEditor 5 Word Count lets you track the number of words and characters.

---

CKEditor 5 Word Count adds the **word/character-count** feature to CKEditor 5 — showing a live count of
words and characters as an editor types, and optionally enforcing/showing limits. It depends on core CKEditor
5.

Use it to show word/character counts in the editor. It is a content-editing/CKEditor feature affecting the
editing UI only; it has no content or access role. Enable it in the CKEditor 5 configuration.
