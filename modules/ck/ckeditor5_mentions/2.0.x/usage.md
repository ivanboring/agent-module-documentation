CKEditor5 Mentions enables smart autocompletion in CKEditor 5: typing a marker like @ or # shows a panel of configurable autocomplete suggestions.

---

CKEditor5 Mentions adds the mention feature to CKEditor 5 — when an editor types a configured marker
(such as `@` or `#`), a panel shows autocomplete suggestions (users, terms, or other configured feeds) to
insert a mention. This supports @-mentioning people or #-tagging topics inside rich-text content. Mention
feeds are configured via the `mention_feed` collection; it provides its own permissions.

Use it to enable mentions/tagging in the editor (community content, internal notes, tagging). It is a
content-editing/CKEditor feature; the inserted mention becomes markup in the content. Consider what the
mention feed exposes — if it suggests usernames, be mindful the autocomplete reveals user names to editors
(fine for internal editors, but scope the feed appropriately). Configure the marker and feed source.
