Asset Fetcher downloads external libraries to serve them locally (with subresource integrity).

---

Asset Fetcher fetches all external library files — downloading external JS/CSS libraries and hosting them locally instead of loading from third-party CDNs, with Sub-Resource Integrity (SRI) checking, improving privacy (no CDN calls) and integrity (verified assets).

It computes SRI hashes over local files (not user-supplied URLs). It's a performance/privacy/developer tool with no content or access role of its own. Supports Drupal 9, 10, and 11.
